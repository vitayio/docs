Vitay records an entry each time someone adds, changes, or deletes a candidate, reference, question set, user, or comment, and each time a candidate or reference completes a form. The Activities log is where you read that history.

<Note>
  The firm-wide **Activities** page is for root recruiters and supervisors. Anyone who can open a candidate or reference can see its **Activities** tab, and everyone can see their own sign-in history under **Account**.
</Note>

## The Activities page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Click **Activities** in the main navigation. The page is headed **Last activities** and shows how many entries match.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the period">
    The page opens on your firm's default list timeframe (one month unless a root recruiter changed it). Click the calendar button to pick a range or a preset: **Last 7 days**, **Month to date**, **Last month**, or **Year to date**. You can't go back before 1 January 2018.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose whose activity to see">
    Use the **Everyone** / **Myself** toggle next to the date picker. **Myself** shows only your own actions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the table">
    Each row has a **Description** of what happened, **New values** and **Old values** where something changed, and how long ago it happened.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![SCREENSHOT(activities-log-page): The Activities page with the date range picker, the Everyone/Myself toggle and the activity table](/images/placeholder.png)

Rows for a new candidate, an added reference, a completed reference, or a new reference request open that candidate when you click them. Other rows don't link anywhere. The refresh button reloads the list and shows **Activities reloaded**.

## What the log covers

Each description names the person and the record, for example *Recruiter Dana Ellis added Priya Shah as a candidate*. Entries cover:

- **Candidates:** created, edited, assigned recruiter changed, status changed, deleted; documents uploaded, downloaded, or deleted; self-reference attempts.
- **References:** added by a candidate or recruiter, completed, edited, deleted, or the reference changing their own contact method.
- **Reference requests:** created, completed by the candidate, or cancelled by an integration.
- **Reminders** sent for references, reference requests, feedback, pre-screenings, and background screenings.
- **Views:** a reference or reference request being opened.
- **Feedback, pre-screening, and background screening** requests created and completed.
- **Question sets, users, and comments:** created, changed, or deleted (users can also be disabled and re-enabled).
- **Sign-in events:** logins, logouts, failed attempts, lockouts, and password changes.

## The Activities tab on a record

Each candidate and reference has its own **Activities** tab with the same kind of entries for that one record. A candidate's tab also lists requests sent to them and every change of assigned recruiter, whether made from the edit form or with the bulk **Assign to recruiter** action.

**Activities** is separate from the **Email activity** and **Text activity** tabs, which show delivery results such as sent, opened, or bounced. See [Email and text delivery](/candidates-reference-requests/email-and-text-delivery).

## Your own account activity

Click the person icon in the top bar to open **Account**, then the **Activities** tab. It lists your recent sign-ins, sign-outs, failed sign-in attempts, lockouts, and password changes.

## Exporting

There's no export of the Activities log. You can export candidates, references, answers, feedback, and pre-screenings instead. See [Download reports](/reports-lead-generation/download-reports).

## The notification bell

The bell in the top bar shows announcements from Vitay, such as release notes and maintenance notices. It doesn't show your team's activity.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    Click the bell. The number on it is how many announcements you haven't read.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read an announcement">
    The **Notifications** panel lists announcements newest first. Click one to expand it. Unread ones are highlighted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the count">
    Click **Mark all as read** at the top of the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See older announcements">
    Click **View all notifications** to open the **Notifications** page, where **All** and **Unread** filter the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![SCREENSHOT(activities-log-notifications-panel): The Notifications panel open from the bell in the top bar, with Mark all as read and View all notifications](/images/placeholder.png)

Announcements can target particular roles, so a root recruiter may see one that a recruiter doesn't. Read status is per person.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't find Activities in the navigation">
    The page is only for root recruiters and supervisors. You can still use the **Activities** tab on any candidate or reference you can open, and your own history under **Account**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The log looks empty">
    Widen the date range (try **Year to date**), then check the **Everyone** / **Myself** toggle.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An entry has no Old values or New values">
    Those columns are only filled when something changed from one value to another, such as a status. Creations, deletions, and completions have nothing to compare.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The bell count comes back after I clear it">
    A new announcement was published since you cleared it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
