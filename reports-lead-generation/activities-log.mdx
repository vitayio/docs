Vitay writes an entry every time someone creates, changes, or deletes something meaningful — a candidate, a reference, a question set, a user, a comment — and every time a candidate or reference completes a form. The Activities log is where you read that history back.

<Note>
  The firm-wide **Activities** page is available to **root recruiters** and **supervisors**. The per-candidate and per-reference **Activities** tabs are visible to anyone who can open that record, and your own sign-in history is always available to you under **Account**.
</Note>

## The Activities page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Click **Activities** in the main navigation. The page is headed **Last activities** — *"Stay on the top of your users activities"* — with a count of how many entries match.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the period">
    The page opens on your firm's default list timeframe — one month back, unless a root recruiter changed it. Use the calendar button to pick a different range, or one of the presets: **Last 7 days**, **Month to date**, **Last month**, **Year to date**. The earliest selectable date is 1 January 2018 and you cannot select a future date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose whose activity to see">
    The **Everyone** / **Myself** toggle sits next to the date picker. **Myself** narrows the log to your own actions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the table">
    Four columns: **Description** (the sentence describing what happened), **New values** and **Old values** (what changed, where the entry recorded a before and after), and the date, shown as a relative time such as *3 hours ago*.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![SCREENSHOT: The Activities page with the date range picker, Everyone/Myself toggle, and the activity table — placeholder](/images/placeholder.png)

Rows for a candidate being created, a reference being added, a reference completing, and a reference request being created are clickable and jump straight to that candidate. Other rows are informational only. The refresh button at the top left re-reads the current page and confirms with **Activities reloaded**.

## What you will see in the log

Descriptions are written out in full, naming the person and the record — for example *"Recruiter **Dana Ellis** added **Priya Shah** as a candidate"*. The entries cover:

- **Candidates** — created, details updated, status changed (from and to, by name), deleted; documents uploaded, downloaded, and deleted.
- **References** — a candidate or a recruiter adding a reference, a reference completing, a reference's details being updated, a reference being deleted, a reference changing their own contact method.
- **Reference requests** — created, and completed by the candidate.
- **Reminders** — reminders sent for references, reference requests, feedback, pre-screenings, and background screenings.
- **Views** — a reference or a reference request being opened and read.
- **Feedback, pre-screening, and background screening** — requests created and completed.
- **Question sets** — created, updated, deleted.
- **Users** — a user added, disabled, or re-enabled.
- **Comments** — added, edited, deleted.
- **Sign-in events** — successful logins, logouts, failed attempts, lockouts, and password changes.

## The Activities tab on a record

Every candidate and every reference has its own **Activities** tab showing the same kind of entries, scoped to that one record.

- On a **candidate**: **Details**, **Activities**, **Comments**, **Email activity**, **Text activity**, **Reference requests**, **Reminders**, **Documents**.
- On a **reference**: **Reference**, **Activities**, **Comments**, **Email activity**, **Text activity**, **Reminders**, **About**.

Use the record's own tab when you are asking "what happened to this candidate", and the firm-wide page when you are asking "what has my team been doing".

<Note>
  **Activities** is not the same as **Email activity** and **Text activity**. Those two tabs show delivery results from the email and SMS providers — sent, opened, bounced, failed. See [Email and text delivery](/candidates-reference-requests/email-and-text-delivery).
</Note>

## Your own account activity

Click the person icon in the top bar (or your name in the navigation) to open **Account**, then the **Activities** tab. It lists your most recent account events — sign-ins, sign-outs, failed sign-in attempts, lockouts, and password changes — as a short history rather than a full log.

## Exporting

The Activities log itself is a screen-only view — there is no CSV of it. What you can export is the underlying data: candidates, references, reference answers, feedback, and pre-screenings, each with a date range and a choice of fields. See [Download and export reports](/reports-lead-generation/download-reports).

## The notification bell

The bell in the top bar is separate from the Activities log. It carries **announcements from Vitay** — release notes, maintenance notices, and product news — not your team's activity.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    Click the bell. A count on the icon shows how many announcements you have not read yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read one">
    The panel, titled **Notifications**, lists announcements newest first with a preview and a date. Click one to expand it in place. Unread ones are highlighted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the count">
    **Mark all as read** at the top of the panel clears the badge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See the whole history">
    **View all notifications** at the bottom opens the full **Notifications** page, where **All** / **Unread** buttons filter the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![SCREENSHOT: The notification bell with its unread count and the Notifications panel open — placeholder](/images/placeholder.png)

Announcements are published by Vitay and targeted at particular roles, so a root recruiter may see a notice that a recruiter does not. An account with nothing published shows **No notifications yet**. Read state is per person — marking one read does not change it for your colleagues.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't find Activities in the navigation">
    The firm-wide page is limited to root recruiters and supervisors. You can still open the **Activities** tab on any candidate or reference you have access to, and your own sign-in history under **Account**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The log looks empty">
    Check the date range first — it opens on your firm's default timeframe, so widen it or use **Year to date**. Then check the **Everyone** / **Myself** toggle.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An entry has no Old values or New values">
    Those columns are only filled for entries that recorded a before-and-after, such as a status change. Creations, deletions, and completions have nothing to compare.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Clicking a row does nothing">
    Only entries tied to a candidate — candidate created, reference added, reference completed, reference request created — navigate anywhere. The rest are read-only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The bell shows a count I can't clear">
    Open the panel and click **Mark all as read**. If the count comes back, a new announcement was published in the meantime.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
