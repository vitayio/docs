The Overview dashboard sums up a period: candidates added, references sent and completed, how long references take, and who on your team did the work. It's the first page you see after signing in.

<Note>
  Everyone can open the dashboard and see their own figures. The **Everyone** scope, the download buttons, and the team charts are for root recruiters and supervisors.
</Note>

## Choose a view

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Click **Overview** in the main navigation. It opens on **References overview**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch views">
    Use the blue dropdown at the top right. It offers **References** and **Leads**, plus **Recruiters feedback**, **Feedback**, and **Pre-screening** when those plugins are on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## References overview

| Tile | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Total candidates** | Candidates added in the period |
| **Credits used** | Billable usage in the period. See [Billing and usage](/account-configuration/billing-and-usage) |
| **References completed / sent** | Completed references, references sent, and the completion rate |
| **Avg. completion time** | Average time from sending a reference to its completion, for references completed in the period. Shows **-** when none completed |

Below the tiles:

- **Daily chart:** **References sent** and **References completed** per day, with a line for candidates added. A reference counts as sent on the day it was created and as completed on the day it was filled, so the columns are independent.
- **Recruiters:** for root recruiters and supervisors, a bar chart of the busiest users (up to ten). Otherwise, a chart of your own figures. **More** opens the full **Recruiters** table.
- **Sub accounts:** only under **All Sub Accounts**, a chart per branch. **More** opens the **Sub accounts** table.
- **Desktop / Mobile:** which devices references were completed on.
- **Last activities:** recent entries from the [Activities log](/reports-lead-generation/activities-log) for the same dates and scope.

## The other views

| View | Tiles | Chart |
|---|---|---|
| **Leads** | **Candidates** and **Clients**, each with its share of all leads | Leads per day |
| **Feedback** | **Total candidates**, **Feedback completed / sent**, **Avg. completion time** | Feedback sent and completed per day |
| **Pre-screening** | **Total candidates**, **Pre-screening completed / sent**, **Avg. completion time** | Pre-screenings sent and completed per day |
| **Recruiters feedback** | None | Your team's scores |

## Dates and scope

Click the calendar button to choose the period, by dragging a range or with **Last 7 days**, **Month to date**, **Last month**, or **Year to date**. The dashboard opens on roughly the last month. The last day of the range is included.

Root recruiters and supervisors also see an **Everyone** / **Myself** toggle. If your account has sub-accounts and you're at the parent account, **All Sub Accounts** appears too and combines every branch.

The dates and scope are in the page address, so a link you share opens on the same view.

## Download a dashboard report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the period and scope">
    Pick your dates and scope.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the download button">
    On **References**, it's a dropdown with **Download CSV** and **Download PDF**. On **Leads**, **Feedback**, and **Pre-screening**, it's a single CSV button, greyed out while **Myself** is selected. **Recruiters feedback** has no download.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The PDF summarizes **Total Candidates**, **Total References**, **References Completed / Sent**, **Avg. Completion Time**, and **Avg. Candidate Score**, then a recruiter breakdown and the desktop/mobile split. The CSV has one row per user with name, email, total candidates, total references, and branch.

For candidate-level and reference-level exports, see [Download reports](/reports-lead-generation/download-reports).

## Why the numbers lag

The header says **Updated every 15 minutes**. The dashboard reads totals that Vitay refreshes on a schedule, so a reference completed in the last few minutes may not be counted yet. Candidate scores update overnight. The candidate's own page always shows the live state.

Since October 2026, **Myself** and the dashboards for recruiters and supervisors count references by the day they were sent and completed, like the firm view. Figures from before that may differ slightly.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A reference I just received isn't counted">
    Wait a few minutes and reload. If it's still missing the next day, write to hello@vitay.io.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The download button is greyed out">
    On **Leads**, **Feedback**, and **Pre-screening**, switch from **Myself** to **Everyone**. If you don't see a download button at all, you aren't a root recruiter or supervisor.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Avg. completion time shows a dash">
    No reference was completed in the period. Widen the date range.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Charts are empty but the tiles have numbers">
    The team bar chart needs the **Everyone** scope. When there's nothing to plot, the card shows an empty state.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The view I want isn't in the dropdown">
    **Recruiters feedback**, **Feedback**, and **Pre-screening** are plugins. A root recruiter turns them on in **Configuration → Plugins**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
