---
title: "Download reports"
description: "Export candidate, reference, lead, and activity data from Vitay as CSV files."
---

Vitay lets you export data from most sections of the platform as CSV files. Use exports to build
your own reports, share data with stakeholders, or archive records outside of Vitay.

<Note>
  You need a recruiter (or higher) role to download reports.
</Note>

## Available report types

| Report | What it contains |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Candidates** | Candidate list with status, score, assigned recruiter, reference totals, and creation date |
| **References (answers per candidate)** | One row per completed reference, with every question and answer |
| **Leads** | Reference contacts, one row per unique email, with lead type, consent timestamp, and consent text |
| **Custom leads** | Contacts captured via your firm's custom lead types (e.g. a second lead category) |
| **Feedback** | Feedback request records with creation and completion dates |
| **Feedback answers** | One row per feedback form with every question and answer (answers are blank for forms not yet completed) |
| **Pre-screenings** | Pre-screening requests with creation dates |
| **Candidates per recruiters** | Summary of candidates, references, and leads grouped by recruiter |
| **Recruiter performance** | Completed and total references by recruiter, with active status |
| **Dashboard** | Recruiter-level summary across the whole firm (or sub-accounts) |
| **Recruiters** | Recruiter list with name, email, and active status |

## Download a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Export modal">
    Click the **download icon** in the top bar. An **Export** modal opens — "Export all of your
    data".

    ![SCREENSHOT: the top bar with the download icon highlighted and the Export modal open — placeholder](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a report type">
    Pick one from the **Tables** dropdown (it starts on "Select a table to export").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date range">
    Use the **Date range** picker. It starts on the last month and matches on the record's creation
    date.

    ![SCREENSHOT: the Export modal with the Tables dropdown, Date range picker and Fields checkboxes — placeholder](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your fields">
    Once you've chosen a table, a **Fields** list of checkboxes appears — tick only the columns you
    want in the file. Leave them all unticked to get the standard set of columns for that table.

    A couple of tables have no field list (they show a dash instead) because their columns are
    fixed: **Reference Answers Per Candidates** and **Candidates Per Recruiter**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by question set (Reference Answers Per Candidates only)">
    When you pick **Reference Answers Per Candidates**, a **Question Set** dropdown appears. Leave
    it on **All question sets**, or pick one to get just those answers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Click **Export**. Vitay generates the CSV file and your browser downloads it automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The Export modal always exports your whole firm's data for the chosen date range — it ignores the
  **Everyone / Myself** toggle you may have set on a list page.
</Note>

A few exports live on their own pages instead of the Export modal: **leads** are downloaded from
the Leads page, and the **dashboard** and **recruiter performance** exports from their respective
pages.

## Export a list straight from the page

The **Candidates** and **References** list pages have their own one-click CSV export, handy when
you've already narrowed the list on screen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter the list first">
    Click the **Filters** (funnel) icon and set the filters you want. See
    [Search for past references](/candidates-reference-requests/search-past-references) for what each
    filter does.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Everyone or Myself">
    Unlike the Export modal, this export **follows the Everyone / Myself toggle**. Switch to
    **Everyone** before exporting if you want the whole firm's records rather than just your own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the download icon">
    With the filter toolbar open, click the **download** icon to get the CSV.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The two list exports don't respect every on-screen filter. The References CSV applies your scope,
  date range, recruiters, types, relationships and question sets — but **not** the reference-status
  or job-title filters, and not the search box. The Candidates CSV applies only the scope and date
  range. If you need an exact match to what's on screen, check the file before sharing it.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  The References export also collapses rows that share an email address, keeping one row per
  contact. That's why the CSV can have fewer rows than the list showed.
</Tip>

## Download an individual candidate's report

Bulk CSVs aside, you can download the report for one candidate or one reference:

- From a **candidate's page**, the download button gives you **PDF** — plus **Word** when your firm
  has .docx downloads enabled.
- From the candidate's **References** tab, you can download per question set in **PDF**, **Word**,
  or **CSV** (one row per reference, with a column per question). Word and CSV each depend on their
  own firm setting.
- From a single completed reference in the sidebar, you get **PDF** (and **Word** if enabled). The
  option only appears once the reference is complete and wasn't declined.

Whether the PDF uses the full or condensed layout, and whether personal details are hidden or a
watermark is applied, comes from your firm's **Settings → Documents** configuration. See
[Read a completed reference](/candidates-reference-requests/read-a-completed-reference) and
[Report settings](/account-configuration/report-settings).

<Tip>
  For trends rather than raw rows — totals, completion times, average scores, desktop vs. mobile —
  use the [Overview dashboard](/reports-lead-generation/overview-dashboard), which has its own
  export.
</Tip>

## Column reference

Most exported columns are self-explanatory. A few worth noting:

- **Score** — a percentage calculated from rating and matrix questions in the question set. Zero
  means no rating questions were answered.
- **Consent Given At** / **Consent Text** — appear on lead and custom-lead exports. They show
  the timestamp when the reference gave lead-gen consent and the exact wording they agreed to.
  See [Lead generation](/reports-lead-generation/lead-generation) for context.
- **Branch name** — the sub-account or hiring firm branch the record belongs to (shown on
  multi-account exports).

## Export a candidate reference-answer report

The **References (answers per candidate)** report is the most detailed export: it creates one row
per completed reference and adds one column per question. This is useful for analysis or for
sharing reference results in spreadsheet form.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the report type">
    Open the **Export** modal from the download icon in the top bar and choose **Reference Answers
    Per Candidates** from the **Tables** dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by question set (optional)">
    If you want answers only from a specific question set, pick it from the **Question Set**
    dropdown. This report's columns are fixed, so there's no **Fields** list to tick.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Click **Export**. Large exports may take a few seconds to generate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The download icon is missing or greyed out">
    CSV downloads are controlled by your firm's **Settings → Documents** configuration:

    - **Allow users to download the CSV reports** controls the download icon in the top bar and on
      the Candidates and References lists. Root recruiters always keep it; everyone else needs this
      setting on.
    - **Allow Users To Download Reports In .Docx** adds the **Word** option to individual report
      downloads. It's off by default, so you'll see only PDF until a root recruiter turns it on.
    - **Allow Candidate Reference Reports in CSV** adds the **CSV** option on a candidate's
      References tab.

    When a format is switched off it's hidden rather than greyed out, so a missing option usually
    means the setting — not a permissions error. Ask a root recruiter to change it, or see
    [Report settings](/account-configuration/report-settings).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The CSV doesn't match what I see on screen">
    The list exports apply some filters and not others — see the warning above. The Export modal
    ignores the **Everyone / Myself** toggle entirely and always covers the whole firm. Re-check the
    date range too: both the modal and the lists start on a default range rather than "all time".
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The downloaded file is empty (only headers)">
    No records match your current filters. Try widening the date range, removing the recruiter
    filter, or confirming that the data you expect exists in Vitay.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A column I expected isn't in the file">
    Some columns only appear on the matching export type (for example, the Consent columns appear
    only on lead exports), and some depend on features enabled for your account. Contact Vitay if
    you think a feature should be on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The report includes more rows than expected">
    Leads and custom-lead exports de-duplicate by email address (one row per unique email). If
    the same contact appears on multiple reference requests, only one row is exported.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
