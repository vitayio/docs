<Update
  label="August 6, 2026"
  description="Job title in templates and a big accessibility upgrade"
  tags={["Feature", "Improvement"]}
  rss={{
    title: "Job title in templates and a big accessibility upgrade",
    description: "Use the job title variable in reference templates, and enjoy a more accessible Vitay — better contrast, full keyboard support, and screen-reader-friendly public forms."
  }}
>
  ### New

  - **Job title in your templates.** Reference and reference-request templates
    now support a `{{job_title}}` variable. Pick it from the variable list in
    **Configuration → Templates** and the position the candidate applied for is
    filled in automatically — so candidates and references always know which
    role the request is about.

  ### Improved

  - **A more accessible Vitay.** We've completed a major accessibility pass
    across the app, with a special focus on the public pages candidates and
    references use. Text contrast now meets WCAG AA throughout (and AAA on the
    public reference flows), every form can be completed with just a keyboard,
    screen readers announce loading states and validation errors, and a
    missing-fields summary points to exactly what's left before submitting.
    Animations are also reduced automatically if your device asks for reduced
    motion.
  - **A refreshed brand teal.** Vitay's default accent color is now a deeper,
    accessible teal — in the app and in your emails, PDF reports, and public
    reference pages. If your firm uses custom branding colors, nothing changes.

  ### Fixed

  - **Adding a candidate who already has a Vitay account.** Creating a candidate
    whose email matched an existing user (for example, adding yourself as a test
    candidate) could fail — it now works reliably.
  - **Question editing glitches.** The question-set and question windows could
    show stale validation — for example, not re-checking the title after
    switching the template toggle, or the answer options after changing a
    question's type. Both now update immediately.
  - **Settings pages after session expiry.** Opening a settings page right after
    your session expired could show an error instead of redirecting to login.
  - **Changing your firm's language.** The new language now applies reliably
    everywhere as soon as you change it.
</Update>

<Update
  label="July 23, 2026"
  description="Share reports with more people and see candidate activity at a glance"
  tags={["Feature", "Improvement"]}
  rss={{
    title: "Share reports with more people and see candidate activity at a glance",
    description: "Add extra report recipients to a candidate, sort your candidate list by last activity, and enjoy a simpler question builder."
  }}
>
  ### New

  - **Send reference reports to more people.** You can now add up to five extra
    email addresses to a candidate, and they'll receive the same PDF reference
    reports as the assigned recruiter — handy for hiring managers or clients who
    need the results without a Vitay login. Turn it on under **Configuration →
    Notifications**, where you can also restrict addable addresses to your
    firm's own email domains for extra safety.
  - **"Last Activity" column on the candidate list.** See at a glance when each
    candidate was last updated — shown as relative time (like "2 hours ago") and
    fully sortable, so the candidates that need attention rise to the top.

  ### Improved

  - **A simpler question builder.** The question category dropdown (Functional,
    Behavioral, Technical) and its column have been removed, along with the
    category score summary in default PDF reports — one less thing to fill in
    when building questionnaires.
</Update>

<Update
  label="July 14, 2026"
  description="Feedback PDFs by email and clearer search feedback"
  tags={["Feature", "Improvement"]}
  rss={{
    title: "Feedback PDFs by email and clearer search feedback",
    description: "Completed feedbacks now arrive as a PDF by email, and search bars show clearer status while you type."
  }}
>
  ### New

  - **Feedback reports now arrive by email.** When a feedback is completed,
    recruiters who have PDF reports enabled now receive the completed feedback
    as a PDF attachment — just like completed references already do. There's no
    new setting to flip: if you've turned on PDF reports for references, feedback
    PDFs are included automatically.

  ### Improved

  - **Clearer search feedback.** Search bars across Vitay now show a spinner
    while a search is running and a "No results found" message when a search
    comes up empty — so you always know whether we're still looking or there's
    simply nothing to show.
</Update>
