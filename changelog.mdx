  ### New

  - **Feedback reports now arrive by email.** When a feedback is completed,
    recruiters who have PDF reports enabled now receive the completed feedback
    as a PDF attachment — just like completed references already do. There's no
    new setting to flip: if you've turned on PDF reports for references, feedback
    PDFs are included automatically.

  ### Improved

  - **Clearer search feedback.** Search bars across Vitay now show a spinner
    while a search is running and a "No results found" message when a search
    comes up empty — so you always know whether we're still looking or there's
    simply nothing to show.
</Update>
