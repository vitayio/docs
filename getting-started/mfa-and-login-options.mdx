Vitay supports two forms of multi-factor authentication (MFA) to add a second layer of security to
your login: a one-time code sent to your phone by SMS, or a time-based code from an authenticator
app. SMS MFA is the option you can turn on yourself from your account settings; authenticator-app
MFA is enabled by Vitay support on request.

<Note>
  Vitay can also enable MFA account-wide for your firm — contact Vitay support at
  [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) to set this up. When it is on, SMS MFA is automatically
  enabled for newly created users, and users cannot turn it off themselves. Existing users are not
  prompted to enroll — they keep their current MFA settings.
</Note>

## SMS / phone MFA

SMS MFA sends a one-time code to your phone number when you log in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Account page">
    Click the person icon in the top bar. This takes you straight to your **Account** page.

    ![SCREENSHOT: the top bar with the person icon highlighted — placeholder](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Security tab">
    On your Account page, open the **Security** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable multi-factor authentication">
    Toggle on **Multi-factor authentication**. The change saves immediately — you'll see a
    "Profile updated" confirmation.

    You need a phone number saved on your account for SMS MFA to work — if you haven't added one,
    do that first on the **Information** tab of the same Account page.

    ![SCREENSHOT: the Security tab with the Multi-factor authentication toggle enabled — placeholder](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Authenticator app (TOTP)

Authenticator-app MFA generates a time-based 6-digit code in an app like Google Authenticator,
Authy, or Microsoft Authenticator. No phone signal is required once set up.

You cannot enable this option yourself — the **Multi-factor Google authentication** toggle on the
Security tab is managed by Vitay. To use an authenticator app, contact Vitay support at
[hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) and ask for it to be enabled on your account. Once it is
enabled, Vitay shows a QR code at login: scan it with your authenticator app, and from then on you
enter the app's current 6-digit code (codes rotate every 30 seconds) each time you log in.

## What to expect at login

If MFA is active on your account, after entering your email and password you will see the
**2FA verification** screen:

- **SMS MFA** — click **Get code**. A code is sent to your registered phone number. Enter the code
  and click **Submit**. After a successful verification, Vitay remembers the browser you used —
  you won't be asked for an SMS code again on later logins from that same device.
- **Authenticator app** — open your authenticator app, enter the current 6-digit code, and click
  **Submit**. Authenticator-app MFA asks for a code at every login.

## Disabling MFA

You can turn off SMS MFA from the same **Security** tab by toggling **Multi-factor
authentication** off — the change saves immediately. If Vitay has enabled MFA account-wide for
your firm, the toggle is locked and you cannot disable it yourself — contact Vitay support at
[hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).

## SSO / Single sign-on

If your organization uses SAML-based single sign-on, authentication is handled by your identity
provider and MFA is typically managed there rather than inside Vitay. See
[SSO / SAML](/security-it/sso-saml) for setup details.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't turn on SMS MFA / I get a phone number error">
    SMS MFA needs a valid phone number saved on your account. Click the person icon in the top
    bar to open your **Account** page, add your phone number on the **Information** tab, then
    return to the **Security** tab and enable the toggle.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My authenticator codes aren't working">
    Authenticator codes are time-sensitive. Make sure your device's clock is set to the correct
    time (ideally synced automatically). If codes still fail, contact Vitay support at
    [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I've lost access to my MFA device">
    Contact Vitay support at [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) so they can reset MFA on
    your account and you can log in again. Root recruiters cannot change another user's MFA
    settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm being asked for MFA but never set it up">
    Your firm may have MFA enabled account-wide, which turns on SMS MFA automatically for newly
    created users. Click **Get code** on the 2FA verification screen to receive a code on your
    saved phone number. If you don't have a phone number on your account, contact Vitay support
    at [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
