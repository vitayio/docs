Vitay supports two forms of multi-factor authentication (MFA) to add a second layer of security to
your login: a one-time code sent to your phone by SMS, or a time-based code from an authenticator
app. SMS MFA is the option you can turn on yourself from your account settings; authenticator-app
MFA is enabled by Vitay support on request.

<Note>
  Vitay can also require MFA for your firm's whole team — contact Vitay support at
  [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) to set this up. When it is on, SMS MFA is automatically
  enabled for newly created users, and users cannot turn it off themselves. The requirement is
  checked every time someone signs in with a password: anyone on the team who has an authenticator
  app or a phone number on their account is asked for a code, even if they never switched MFA on
  themselves. Team members with neither can still sign in without a code, so add a phone number to
  every account you want covered.
</Note>

## SMS / phone MFA

SMS MFA sends a one-time code to your phone number when you log in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Account page">
    Click the person icon in the top bar. This takes you straight to your **Account** page.

    ![SCREENSHOT: the top bar with the person icon highlighted — placeholder](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Security tab">
    On your Account page, open the **Security** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable multi-factor authentication">
    Toggle on **Multi-factor authentication**. The change saves immediately — you'll see a
    "Profile updated" confirmation.

    You need a phone number saved on your account for SMS MFA to work — if you haven't added one,
    do that first on the **Information** tab of the same Account page.

    ![SCREENSHOT: the Security tab with the Multi-factor authentication toggle enabled — placeholder](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Authenticator app (TOTP)

Authenticator-app MFA generates a time-based 6-digit code in an app like Google Authenticator,
Authy, or Microsoft Authenticator. No phone signal is required once set up.

You cannot enable this option yourself — the **Multi-factor Google authentication** toggle on the
Security tab is managed by Vitay. To use an authenticator app, contact Vitay support at
[hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) and ask for it to be enabled on your account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Stay signed in while support turns it on">
    Keep Vitay open while support enables the option. From your next sign-in onward, Vitay asks
    for the authenticator code, so scan the QR code before you sign out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the QR code">
    Click the person icon in the top bar, open the **Security** tab, and refresh the page. The QR
    code appears under the **Multi-factor Google authentication** card.

    ![SCREENSHOT: the Security tab showing the QR code under the Multi-factor Google authentication card — placeholder](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan it with your authenticator app">
    Add a new account in your authenticator app and scan the code. The app then shows a 6-digit
    code that changes every 30 seconds — that is the code you enter each time you sign in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to expect at login

If MFA is active on your account, or your firm requires it, you will see the **2FA verification**
screen after entering your email and password. Vitay decides which method to ask for: the
authenticator app if you have one set up, otherwise a text message to your phone.

- **SMS MFA** — click **Get code**. A code is sent to your registered phone number. Enter the code
  and click **Submit**.
- **Authenticator app** — open your authenticator app, enter the current 6-digit code, and click
  **Submit**.

Vitay asks for a code on **every** password sign-in, on every device. There is no "remember this
device" option.

Finish the code step within 10 minutes of entering your password. If you take longer, Vitay sends
you back to the login page to start again.

Wrong codes count towards the same limit as wrong passwords: after 10 failed attempts in a row,
Vitay locks the account (see [Logging in](/getting-started/logging-in#troubleshooting) for how to
unlock it).

## Disabling MFA

You can turn off SMS MFA from the same **Security** tab by toggling **Multi-factor
authentication** off — the change saves immediately. If Vitay has enabled MFA account-wide for
your firm, the toggle is locked and you cannot disable it yourself — contact Vitay support at
[hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).

## SSO / Single sign-on

If your organization uses SAML-based single sign-on, authentication is handled by your identity
provider and MFA is typically managed there rather than inside Vitay. Vitay does not ask for its
own MFA code when you sign in through SSO, even if your firm requires MFA. See
[SSO / SAML](/security-it/sso-saml) for setup details.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't turn on SMS MFA / I get a phone number error">
    SMS MFA needs a valid phone number saved on your account. Click the person icon in the top
    bar to open your **Account** page, add your phone number on the **Information** tab, then
    return to the **Security** tab and enable the toggle.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My authenticator codes aren't working">
    Authenticator codes are time-sensitive. Make sure your device's clock is set to the correct
    time (ideally synced automatically). If codes still fail, contact Vitay support at
    [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) before you use up your attempts: 10 wrong codes or
    passwords in a row lock your account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I've lost access to my MFA device">
    Contact Vitay support at [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) so they can reset MFA on
    your account and you can log in again. Root recruiters cannot change another user's MFA
    settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm being asked for MFA but never set it up">
    Your firm probably requires MFA for its whole team. When it does, Vitay asks for a code from
    anyone with a phone number on their account, even if they never switched MFA on. Click
    **Get code** on the 2FA verification screen to receive a code on your saved phone number. If
    that number is out of date, contact Vitay support at
    [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Vitay used to remember my device and now asks for a code every time">
    That's expected. Vitay no longer skips the code step on devices you've used before: every
    password sign-in needs a code if MFA applies to you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I signed out before scanning the authenticator QR code">
    Once the authenticator option is on, you need a code from the app to sign in, and the QR code
    only shows inside Vitay. Contact Vitay support at [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) so
    they can reset the option and you can sign in and scan the code.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
