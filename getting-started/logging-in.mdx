All recruiters and admins sign in through their firm's Vitay environment. The address depends on
your region:

- **United States / global** — [references.vitay.io](https://references.vitay.io)
- **Canada** — [references.vitay.ca](https://references.vitay.ca)
- **United Kingdom** — [references.vitay.co.uk](https://references.vitay.co.uk)

You need the email address and password that were sent to you when your account was created. If
you're not sure which environment your firm is on, see
[Data residency](/security-it/data-and-privacy) or contact
[hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your Vitay login page">
    Open your firm's Vitay address (see above) in your browser. You will see the Vitay login page.

    ![The Vitay login page with email and password fields](/images/login.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email and password">
    Type your email address and password, then click **Sign in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete MFA if prompted">
    If multi-factor authentication is enabled on your account, you will be taken to the
    **2FA verification** screen. For SMS MFA, click **Get code**, enter the code sent to your
    phone, then click **Submit**. If you use an authenticator app, enter the current 6-digit code
    from the app and click **Submit**.
    See [Multi-factor authentication & login options](/getting-started/mfa-and-login-options) for setup details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reset your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click 'Forgot password'">
    On the login page, click the **Forgot password** link below the password field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Type your Vitay email address and submit the form. Vitay sends you a password-reset email with
    a link.

    <Note>
      Password reset emails are rate-limited. If you request more than two resets within a
      three-hour window, you will need to wait before requesting another one.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the link and set a new password">
    Click the link in the reset email and choose a new password. Passwords must be at least
    8 characters and include an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number, and a special
    character. You can't reuse your current password or any of your previous five.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Password and session rules

These rules apply to every recruiter account:

- **Password complexity** — at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter,
  a number, and a special character.
- **Password history** — a new password can't match your current password or any of the
  previous five you've used.
- **Lockout** — 10 consecutive failed sign-in attempts lock the account (see
  [Why was my account locked?](#troubleshooting) below).
- **Reset rate limit** — at most two password-reset emails per address in any three-hour window.
- **Password expiry** — only applies when your firm has turned on **Require passwords update**.
  When it's on, passwords expire after 30 days by default; a root recruiter can set anywhere
  from 1 to 365 days. Users who sign in through SSO are never asked to change an expired
  password.
- **Session length** — a sign-in lasts up to 24 hours before you need to sign in again. Your
  firm can also log you out sooner after a period of inactivity with the **IDLE** setting
  (off by default, 15 minutes when enabled) — see
  [Firm configuration](/account-configuration/firm-configuration).
- **Email domains** — Vitay doesn't accept Tutanota or Proton email addresses for user
  accounts; use your work address instead.

## SSO users

If your organization uses single sign-on (SAML/SSO), click **Sign in with SSO** on the login page,
enter your work email address, and continue. You will complete authentication through your identity
provider rather than entering a Vitay password.

If SSO is required for your organization and you try to sign in with an email and password instead,
Vitay shows an error asking you to use SSO. For setup and troubleshooting, see
[SSO / SAML](/security-it/sso-saml).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't remember my password">
    Use the **Forgot password** link on the login page. If you don't receive the reset email
    within a few minutes, check your spam folder. The email comes from Vitay's notification address.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why was my account locked?">
    There are two reasons an account gets locked:

    - **Too many failed sign-ins.** Vitay locks the account after 10 consecutive failed
      attempts.
    - **Inactivity.** If your firm has turned on **Disable inactive accounts**, Vitay
      periodically locks any non-root user who hasn't signed in for a month — even if they
      never entered a wrong password.

    Either way, you can unlock the account yourself: use the **Forgot password** link and
    complete the password reset — finishing the reset unlocks the account. Alternatively, a
    root recruiter can use the **Unlock and Reset password** button on your user page (see
    [Manage users](/account-configuration/manage-users)), or you can contact Vitay support at
    [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm being asked to verify my identity but I don't have the app">
    If you've lost access to your MFA device, contact Vitay support at
    [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) so they can reset MFA on your account and you can
    log in again. Root recruiters cannot change another user's MFA settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm asked to change my password when I log in">
    Your firm has turned on **Require passwords update**, which expires passwords
    on a schedule (30 days by default; a root recruiter can set 1–365 days). When
    your password expires, Vitay takes you to the change-password screen before
    you can continue, and the new password can't match your current one or any
    of your previous five. If you sign in through your organization's SSO, this
    doesn't apply to you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I see a 'password reset rate limit' message">
    You can request up to two password resets within any three-hour period. Wait until the window
    expires, then try again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
