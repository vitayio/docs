Recruiters and admins sign in at their region's Vitay address:

- United States and global: [references.vitay.io](https://references.vitay.io)
- Canada: [references.vitay.ca](https://references.vitay.ca)
- United Kingdom: [references.vitay.co.uk](https://references.vitay.co.uk)

<Note>
  You need the email address and password from your account invitation. If you're not sure which
  region your firm is on, see [Data residency](/security-it/data-and-privacy) or contact
  [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
</Note>

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Vitay login page">
    Go to your region's address from the list above.

    ![SCREENSHOT(logging-in-form): The Vitay login page with the Email and Password fields, the Sign in button and the Sign in with SSO button](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email and password">
    Type your email and password, then click **Sign in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your MFA code if asked">
    If multi-factor authentication applies to you, the **2FA verification** screen opens. For a
    text-message code, click **Get code**, type the code you receive, and click **Submit**. For an
    authenticator app, type the current code from the app and click **Submit**. You have 10
    minutes to finish this step. See
    [Multi-factor authentication & login options](/getting-started/mfa-and-login-options).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reset your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Forgot password">
    On the login page, click **Forgot password** under the password field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request the reset email">
    Type your Vitay email address and click **Submit**. If the address matches an account, Vitay
    emails you a reset link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a new password">
    Open the link within 1 hour and choose a new password. Each link works once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Password and session rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Password strength | At least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a special character. |
| Password history | A new password can't match your current one or your previous five. |
| Lockout | 10 failed sign-in attempts in a row lock the account. Wrong MFA codes count too. |
| Reset emails | Two per email address in any three-hour window. Each link expires after 1 hour. |
| Burst limits | Many sign-in or reset attempts in a short time, from one network or for one address, are paused for a few minutes. |
| Session length | Up to 24 hours. Your firm can also sign people out after inactivity with the **IDLE** setting (15 minutes when turned on). |
| Password expiry | Only when your firm turns on **Require passwords update**: 30 days by default, adjustable from 1 to 365. Never applies to SSO sign-ins. |
| Email domains | Tutanota and Proton addresses can't be used for Vitay user accounts. |

The strength rules apply whenever a password is set. A password set before these rules existed
keeps working until you change it.

When a root recruiter deactivates you (or your identity provider does, through SCIM), you're signed
out on your next action and can't sign in again until you're reactivated.

## SSO users

If your organization uses single sign-on, click **Sign in with SSO** on the login page, enter your
work email and click **Continue**. You then sign in through your identity provider instead of with
a Vitay password. If your firm requires SSO, a password sign-in is refused with *"Please login with
SSO"*. See [Single sign-on (SAML)](/security-it/sso-saml).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't remember my password">
    Use **Forgot password** on the login page. If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes,
    check your spam folder.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why was my account locked?">
    Either there were 10 failed sign-in attempts in a row (wrong passwords and wrong MFA codes both
    count), or your firm has turned on **Lock inactive users**, which locks users other than root
    recruiters who haven't signed in for a month.

    To unlock it, complete a password reset with **Forgot password**. A root recruiter can also
    unlock you from the Users page (see [Manage users](/account-configuration/manage-users)), or
    you can contact [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I've lost access to my MFA device">
    Contact [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) so support can reset MFA on your account. Root
    recruiters can't change another user's MFA settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm asked to change my password when I log in">
    This happens on your first sign-in, and when your password has expired under your firm's
    **Require passwords update** setting. Choose a new password that you haven't used in your last
    five. SSO sign-ins are never asked.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I see a password reset rate limit message">
    You can request two resets in any three-hour period. The message tells you how long to wait.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My reset link says it's invalid or expired">
    Links expire after 1 hour and stop working once used. Click **Forgot password** to request a
    new one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I see a 'Too many requests' error">
    There were many sign-in or reset attempts in a short time, either for your address or from your
    network (a shared office connection, for example). Wait a few minutes and try again. Your
    account isn't locked.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I was signed out and can't sign back in">
    Your account has probably been deactivated. You may see *"This account is currently
    inactive"*. Ask a root recruiter at your firm to reactivate you (see
    [Manage users](/account-configuration/manage-users)).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
