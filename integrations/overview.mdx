Vitay integrates with popular applicant tracking systems (ATS) so your team can run reference checks alongside your ATS and get completed reference reports back onto the candidate's record.

<Note>
  Setting up an integration requires **root recruiter** access in Vitay. The exact steps vary by ATS — see the article for your platform below.
</Note>


![The Integrations gallery in Configuration](/images/integrations.png)

## What integrations do

Depending on the platform, a connected integration can:

- **Import candidates** from your ATS into Vitay — in one click, or automatically as candidates become ready for reference checks.
- **Send reference requests** for imported candidates without re-entering their details.
- **Get completed reference reports back into your ATS** — some integrations push the completed report to the candidate's record automatically, others add it through a manual upload action in Vitay. See the article for your platform for exactly what it supports.

Connection credentials are stored on your Vitay account, so day-to-day use requires no re-authentication.
