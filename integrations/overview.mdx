Connect Vitay to your applicant tracking system (ATS) to bring candidates into Vitay and get completed references back onto the candidate's ATS record.

<Note>
  Connecting an integration requires the root recruiter role in Vitay. The steps differ by ATS, so follow the article for your platform below.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open the **Configuration** area and select **Integrations** in the left menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your ATS">
    Click your ATS's card and follow the article for that platform. A connected integration shows an **Active** badge on its card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![SCREENSHOT(overview-integrations-gallery): The Integrations page in Configuration, showing a card for each ATS](/images/placeholder.png)

## What integrations do

What a connection can do depends on the platform:

- Import candidates from your ATS into Vitay, on demand or automatically when they're flagged or reach a set stage.
- Send reference requests to imported candidates without retyping their details.
- Send results back to your ATS, either automatically as references complete or with an upload action in Vitay.

## Supported integrations

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Bullhorn" icon="bullseye" href="/integrations/bullhorn">
    Push candidates and reference reports to Bullhorn, and request references from inside Bullhorn.
  </Card>
  <Card title="eRecruit" icon="rotate" href="/integrations/erecruit">
    Scheduled sync that imports candidates from eRecruit and sends them reference requests.
  </Card>
  <Card title="JobAdder" icon="briefcase" href="/integrations/jobadder">
    Adds a Vitay action to JobAdder candidate records.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Greenhouse" icon="seedling" href="/integrations/greenhouse">
    Imports candidates you flag in Greenhouse and posts progress notes to their activity feed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="JobDiva" icon="users" href="/integrations/jobdiva">
    Imports candidates you flag in JobDiva and sends each one a reference request.
  </Card>
  <Card title="TargetRecruit" icon="cloud" href="/integrations/targetrecruit">
    Exchanges candidates and references with TargetRecruit and files completed reference PDFs on the candidate.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The Integrations page also lists Workable, which doesn't have its own article yet. Contact [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) for help setting it up, or to ask about an ATS that isn't listed.

## Integration limit

Accounts start with a limit of one active integration. Once you reach it, the page shows "You have reached your integrations limit" and the other cards stop opening. Contact [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) to have the limit raised.
