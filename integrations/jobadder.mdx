Connect Vitay to JobAdder to add a **View candidate** action to JobAdder candidate records. It opens the candidate in Vitay, importing them if needed, so you can send a reference request.

<Note>
  Connecting JobAdder requires the root recruiter role in Vitay and a JobAdder login that can approve the connection. You don't need API credentials; Vitay supplies them.
</Note>

## What the integration does

- View candidate action. Vitay adds a **View candidate** action to the Candidate record in JobAdder.
- Import on demand. Using the action brings the JobAdder candidate into Vitay, or opens their existing Vitay record.
- Push candidates to JobAdder. Create a JobAdder candidate from a Vitay candidate.
- Upload reference reports. Attach a reference PDF to the candidate's JobAdder profile.

## Connect JobAdder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the JobAdder card">
    In the **Configuration** area, select **Integrations** and click the **JobAdder** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Start">
    Click **Start**. Vitay sends you to JobAdder's sign-in page.

    ![SCREENSHOT(jobadder-connect-modal): The JobAdder dialog with the Start button](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve in JobAdder">
    Sign in to JobAdder and approve the connection. You return to Vitay, and the **View candidate** action is added to your JobAdder account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use the integration

- In JobAdder, open a candidate and choose **View candidate**. The candidate opens in Vitay, where you can send a reference request. If Vitay shows **Get candidate data**, click it to import the candidate.
- In Vitay, use the **Synchronize** menu on the candidate page or on a completed reference to push the candidate or a report to JobAdder.

## How often it syncs

Nothing runs on a schedule. A candidate is pulled from JobAdder when someone uses **View candidate**, and pushes and uploads happen when you trigger them. A change made in JobAdder reaches Vitay the next time you open the candidate through the action.

## Disconnect JobAdder

A root recruiter clicks the **JobAdder** card and chooses **Delete Integration**. Vitay also removes the **View candidate** action from JobAdder. If JobAdder has already revoked Vitay's access, the action can't be removed for you: delete it from your JobAdder partner actions, or contact [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The View candidate action is missing in JobAdder">
    The action is added when you approve the connection. If it's missing, the connection may not have finished. Disconnect and connect again from **Integrations**, or contact [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A reference report won't upload to JobAdder">
    The candidate must be linked to JobAdder first, either imported through **View candidate** or pushed from Vitay.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Authorization errors">
    Vitay renews its JobAdder access automatically. If errors persist, the access may have been revoked in JobAdder. Disconnect and connect again, or contact [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
