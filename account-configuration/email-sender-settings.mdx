Every email Vitay sends on your behalf — reference requests, reminders, and thank-you notes — shows a sender name and email address in the recipient's inbox. You can choose whether those come from the individual recruiter's identity or from a shared, firm-wide value.

<Note>
  You need a **root recruiter** role to change email sender settings. Changes take effect for all future outgoing emails as soon as they are saved.
</Note>

## How sender identity works

Sender identity is controlled by the **Email Sender Identity** card in **Settings → Configuration**. It has two independent choices:

| Control | Choices | What recipients see |
|---|---|---|
| **From: Address** | **Recruiter's email** (default) or **Custom address** | The email address in the From line |
| **From: Name** | **Recruiter's name** (default) or **Custom name** | The display name in the From line |

Because the two are independent, you can mix them: for example, show each recruiter's real name but send from one shared company address.

## Which setup should you choose?

### Personalised — recruiter's email and name

Both controls set to the recruiter's own value. Emails arrive looking as though they came directly
from the recruiter managing that candidate.

- **Good for** firms where candidates already have a relationship with a named recruiter, and where
  personal outreach lifts response rates. Recipients are less likely to ignore a familiar sender.
- **Consider** that every recruiter's email domain needs to be authenticated (see below) or messages
  may land in spam, and that replies land in individual inboxes rather than a shared one.

### Centralised — one custom address and name

Both controls set to a custom value, e.g. `references@yourcompany.com` with the name
"Acme Talent Team". Every email looks the same regardless of who is assigned.

- **Good for** a consistent, branded experience, teams with recruiter turnover, firms that want
  replies in a shared inbox, and IT teams with strict email governance.
- **Consider** that it feels less personal — but it's far easier to authenticate and maintain,
  because there's only one domain to configure.

### Mixed

Use the recruiter's address with a custom display name, or the reverse. Less common, but useful when
replies should reach the recruiter while the brand name stays consistent.

### Using a Vitay-hosted address

If you don't have a company address you want to send from, you can leave the custom address as a
Vitay-hosted one — new accounts start with `recruitment@vitay.io` and the sender name "Recruitment".

- **Pros:** nothing to configure, and Vitay handles authentication and deliverability for you.
- **Cons:** the address doesn't match your company's domain, which can look less familiar to
  candidates and references.

It's a good starting point for smaller teams; most established firms eventually move to their own
domain.

## Where replies go

The From address is not necessarily where replies land. Reply-to routing is controlled separately,
on the same **Settings → Configuration** tab:

| Setting | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Redirect support emails to assigned user** (on by default) | Replies go to the recruiter assigned to that candidate — their own address and name. |
| **Primary reply email** / **Primary Reply Name** | Used when the redirect setting is off. Every reply goes to this one shared address instead. |

So a firm can send everything from one branded address and still have each reply reach the right
recruiter — or switch the redirect off to funnel all replies into a shared inbox.

## Change the sender address or name

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Configuration settings">
    Go to **Settings → Configuration** and find the **Email Sender Identity** card.

    ![The email (sender) settings page](/images/email-sender-settings.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the From: Address">
    Next to **From: Address**, click either **Recruiter's email** or **Custom address**. The choice saves as soon as you click it.

    If you choose **Custom address**, an input appears (for example, `noreply@yourcompany.com`). Type the address, then click the inline **save** icon next to the field to store it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the From: Name">
    Next to **From: Name**, click either **Recruiter's name** or **Custom name** — again, the choice saves on click.

    If you choose **Custom name**, type the display name (for example, "Acme Recruiting Team") in the input that appears and click its inline **save** icon.

    ![SCREENSHOT: The Email Sender Identity card with the From: Address and From: Name segmented choices and a custom input with its inline save icon — placeholder](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Deliverability and DMARC

If you set a custom From address on a domain you own (e.g. `hiring@yourcompany.com`), your domain's DMARC policy may cause emails to land in spam or be rejected outright — unless Vitay is authorised to send on your domain's behalf.

### The three standards, in plain language

Three DNS-based standards work together to prove an email really came from your domain:

- **SPF (Sender Policy Framework)** — a DNS entry listing which mail servers are allowed to send
  email for your domain. Vitay's sending infrastructure has to be on that list.
- **DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail)** — a cryptographic signature added to every message, proving
  it came from an authorised sender and hasn't been altered on the way. Vitay supplies the DKIM
  entries you publish.
- **DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting & Conformance)** — builds on the other
  two. It tells receiving mail servers what to do with a message that fails those checks: reject
  it, send it to spam, or let it through — and can send you reports on what's happening.

You don't need to write any of these by hand. When you add a domain in Vitay, the records you need
are generated for you; your job is to publish them in your DNS.

### Add and verify your domain

You can set this up yourself from **Settings → Developers**, under **DMARC Protection**:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your domain">
    Enter your domain in the **Domain name** field and click **Add Domain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the DNS records">
    Vitay generates a table of DNS records (**Type**, **Host**, **Value**) for your domain. Ask your IT team or domain registrar to publish these records in your domain's DNS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Click **Check DMARC status**. Vitay checks each record and shows a green "valid" indicator once the records are verified.

    ![SCREENSHOT: The DMARC Protection section on Settings → Developers with the generated DNS records table and Check DMARC status button — placeholder](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  DMARC setup requires access to your company's DNS. It is a one-time step per domain. If you get stuck, contact **hello@vitay.io**.
</Note>

<Note>
  Adding and verifying a domain here does not change your From address. It authorises the domain so
  mail sent from it is trusted — you still choose the address itself with **From: Address** on the
  Configuration tab.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="References say emails are landing in their spam folder">
    This is most commonly a deliverability issue. If you are using a custom From address, confirm that the DNS records generated under **Settings → Developers → DMARC Protection** have been published and show as valid. If you are using recruiters' own emails, ask your IT team whether your company's email domain has a restrictive DMARC policy that prevents third-party sending.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I set a custom From address but outgoing emails still show the recruiter's address">
    Check that **From: Address** is set to **Custom address** (not **Recruiter's email**) and that you clicked the inline **save** icon after typing the address — the custom value is only stored when you save it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Replies are going to the wrong inbox">
    Replies are not controlled by the From address. On **Settings → Configuration**, check
    **Redirect support emails to assigned user**: when it's on, replies go to the recruiter assigned
    to that candidate; when it's off, they all go to the **Primary reply email** instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="We haven't set a custom address — what are emails sent from?">
    New accounts start with a Vitay-hosted address (`recruitment@vitay.io`, sender name
    "Recruitment"), which is used whenever **From: Address** is set to **Custom address** and you
    haven't replaced it. That works out of the box with no DNS setup.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
