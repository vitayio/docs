---
title: "Email sender settings"
description: "Control the From name and email address that references and candidates see when Vitay sends them emails."
---

Emails Vitay sends for you, such as reference requests and reminders, show a sender name and address in the recipient's inbox. You choose whether those come from each recruiter or from one firm-wide value.

<Note>
  You need the root recruiter role. Changes apply to emails sent after they are saved.
</Note>

## How sender identity works

The **Email Sender Identity** card on the **Configuration** tab in Settings has two independent choices:

| Control | Choices | What recipients see |
|---|---|---|
| **From: Address** | **Recruiter's email** (default) or **Custom address** | The address in the From line |
| **From: Name** | **Recruiter's name** (default) or **Custom name** | The display name in the From line |

You can mix them, for example each recruiter's name with one shared company address.

## Which setup should you choose?

### Personalised: recruiter's email and name

Emails look like they come straight from the recruiter managing the candidate. This works well when candidates already know a named recruiter. Every recruiter's email domain needs to be authenticated (see [Deliverability and DMARC](#deliverability-and-dmarc)) or messages may land in spam.

### Centralised: one custom address and name

For example `references@yourcompany.com` with the name "Acme Talent Team". Every email looks the same whoever is assigned. It's less personal, but there's only one domain to authenticate, and it holds up when recruiters leave.

### Mixed

The recruiter's address with a custom name, or the reverse. Useful when the brand name should stay consistent but the address should be the recruiter's.

### Vitay-hosted address

New accounts start with the custom address `recruitment@vitay.io` and the name "Recruitment". It needs no DNS setup, but it doesn't match your company's domain, so it can look less familiar to recipients.

## Where replies go

The From address doesn't decide where replies land. Replies to candidate and reference emails go to the recruiter assigned to the candidate. This isn't a setting on the tab; if replies should go to one shared inbox instead, contact **hello@vitay.io**.

## Which sender your team sees

Emails to your own team, such as **Reference completed**, **Candidate submitted their references**, **Reference awaiting your review**, **Feedback completed**, **Pre-screening completed** and the **Send test** button on the **Emails** tab, also carry your firm's identity:

| Your **From: Address** choice | Team emails are sent from |
|---|---|
| **Recruiter's email** | Your **Primary reply email** and **Primary Reply Name** |
| **Custom address** | The custom address and name |

Replies to team emails go to your **Primary reply email**. The exception is **Reference awaiting your review**: it shows as "*Candidate name* via *Your firm*" and replying reaches the candidate. Until you change **Primary reply email**, it is Vitay's support address (hello@vitay.io). Password resets and other account emails always come from Vitay.

If team emails come from your own domain, authenticate it under [Deliverability and DMARC](#deliverability-and-dmarc), or mail clients may show them as sent "via" another domain or file them as spam.

## Change the sender address or name

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Configuration tab">
    In the Configuration area, click **Settings**, then the **Configuration** tab. Scroll to the **Email Sender Identity** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the From: Address">
    Next to **From: Address**, click **Recruiter's email** or **Custom address**. The choice saves when you click it.

    For **Custom address**, type the address in the field that appears. It saves when you stop typing, and **Saved** shows under the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the From: Name">
    Next to **From: Name**, click **Recruiter's name** or **Custom name**. For **Custom name**, type the display name in the field that appears.

    ![SCREENSHOT(email-sender-settings-identity-card): The Email Sender Identity card with the From: Address and From: Name choices and the custom address field](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Deliverability and DMARC

If you send from a domain you own (for example `hiring@yourcompany.com`), your domain's DMARC policy can send those emails to spam or reject them unless Vitay is authorised to send for that domain.

Three DNS standards prove an email came from your domain:

- **SPF** lists the mail servers allowed to send for your domain.
- **DKIM** adds a signature to each message showing it came from an authorised sender and wasn't changed.
- **DMARC** tells receiving servers what to do with mail that fails those checks.

You don't write these records yourself. Vitay generates them and you publish them in your DNS.

### Add and verify your domain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Developers tab">
    In Settings, click the **Developers** tab and find **DMARC Protection**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your domain">
    Type your domain in **Domain name** and click **Add Domain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the DNS records">
    Vitay lists the DNS records for the domain (**Type**, **Host**, **Value**). Ask your IT team or domain registrar to publish them. The download button next to the domain gives you the records as a CSV.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Click the refresh button next to the domain (tooltip **Check DMARC status**). A green check mark appears next to the domain once the records are verified.

    ![SCREENSHOT(email-sender-settings-dmarc): The DMARC Protection card on the Developers tab with a domain added and its Type, Host and Value records listed](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

Adding a domain doesn't change your From address. It only authorises the domain; you still pick the address with **From: Address**. If you get stuck, contact **hello@vitay.io**.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="References say our emails land in spam">
    If you use a custom From address, check that its domain shows a green check mark under **DMARC Protection** on the **Developers** tab. If you use recruiters' own addresses, ask your IT team whether your domain's DMARC policy blocks third-party sending, and add the domain under **DMARC Protection**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Emails to my team show 'via' another domain or land in spam">
    Team emails come from the address in [Which sender your team sees](#which-sender-your-team-sees). If that address is on your domain, add and verify the domain under **DMARC Protection**. If it is hello@vitay.io, you haven't set a **Primary reply email** or custom **From: Address** yet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I set a custom From address but emails still show the recruiter's address">
    Check that **From: Address** is set to **Custom address** and that the status under the field says **Saved**. If it says **Not saved**, the address was rejected; check it for typos.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Replies are going to the wrong inbox">
    Replies to candidate and reference emails go to the candidate's assigned recruiter, whatever the From address. Check who is assigned to the candidate (see [Manage users](/account-configuration/manage-users#change-the-assigned-recruiter-for-a-candidate)).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
