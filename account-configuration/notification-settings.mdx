title: "Notification settings"
description: "Choose when and who Vitay notifies about completed references, feedback, leads, and unresponsive contacts."
---

Vitay can send your team email notifications at key moments in the reference-checking process. You control exactly which events trigger a notification and which team members receive it.

<Note>
  You need a **root recruiter** role to change notification settings. Changes apply to all future activity in your account.
</Note>

## Two types of notifications

Both live on the **Settings → Notification** tab:

- **Event notification toggles** — on/off switches that fire automatically (e.g., "notify the assigned user when a reference is completed").
- **Custom notification cards** — per-event cards that let you choose *who* receives each workflow email (assigned recruiter, the account's root email, or a custom email list).

The same tab also holds the **Auto reminders**, **Usage Notifications** and **Additional report
recipients** cards, covered below.

## Turn off feedback reminders on their own

The **Auto reminders** card at the top of the tab controls the reminder sequence for candidates and
references. Inside it, **Feedback reminders** is a separate switch:

- Leave it **on** (the default) and feedback recipients get the same reminder sequence as everyone
  else.
- Turn it **off** and recipients get the original feedback request only — no follow-ups — while
  reference reminders carry on as normal.

<Note>
  **Feedback reminders** only appears when **Auto reminders** is on and at least one feedback type is
  enabled for your firm. See [Candidate feedback](/feedback/candidate-feedback).
</Note>

## Configure event notification toggles

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Notification settings">
    Go to **Settings → Notification**.

    ![The notification settings page](/images/notification-settings.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable or disable each toggle">
    Turn a toggle **on** to activate that notification, **off** to suppress it. Each toggle saves as soon as you flip it — there is no Save button. Common toggles include:

    | Toggle | What it does |
    |---|---|
    | **Reference request completed** | Notify the assigned user when a candidate has submitted all their references |
    | **Candidate: Reference is completed** | Notify the candidate when one of their references finishes |
    | **Candidate: Reference has been added** | Notify the candidate when a user adds a new reference on their behalf |
    | **Candidate reference pending notification** | Notify the candidate when their reference is still pending |
    | **Candidate: Notify Reference Contact Errors** | Notify the candidate when Vitay cannot deliver an email or text to a reference |
    | **Email errors notification** | Notify the assigned recruiter when an outgoing email cannot be delivered |
    | **Text errors notification** | Notify the assigned recruiter when an outgoing SMS cannot be delivered |
    | **IP address conflict** | Alert the assigned recruiter if a candidate and reference share the same IP address |
    | **Send email notification with PDF report** | Attach a PDF reference report to the recruiter's completion notification |

  </Step>
</Steps>

### Delivery failures

**Email errors notification** is on by default; **Text errors notification** is off by default. Turn
the text one on if your firm sends reference requests by SMS and you want to hear about undelivered
messages. **Candidate: Notify Reference Contact Errors** is the candidate-facing equivalent — it
covers both failed emails and failed texts to their references, and is off by default. See
[Email and text delivery](/candidates-reference-requests/email-and-text-delivery).

### IP address conflicts

**IP address conflict** (on by default) emails the assigned recruiter when a candidate and one of
their references submit from the same IP address. A related **IP conflict banner** switch adds a
warning banner to the report itself — you'll find it nested under **Send email notification with PDF
report**, and it only appears while that toggle is on. For what the alert means and how to clear it,
see [IP conflicts and reference edits](/candidates-reference-requests/ip-conflicts-and-reference-edits).

### Attach the PDF report to the completion email

**Send email notification with PDF report** (off by default) attaches the completed reference report
as a PDF to the recruiter's completion notification, so the report arrives in the inbox rather than
needing a trip back into Vitay. Leave it off if your policy is that reports should only be read
inside the platform.

## Additional report recipients

**Additional report recipients** lets your users add extra email addresses to an individual
candidate. Those addresses then receive the same confidential reference report emails as the
assigned recruiter — useful for a hiring manager or client contact who should see the result without
a Vitay login.

- A candidate can have at most **five** report recipients.
- Turn on **Restrict to your organization's domains** to only accept addresses whose domain matches
  one already used by an active recruiter in your firm. Anything else is rejected when it's added.

<Warning>
  Report recipients receive confidential reference content by email. Use the domain restriction if
  reports should never leave your organisation. See
  [Share reports](/candidates-reference-requests/share-reports).
</Warning>

## Usage notifications

**Usage Notifications** warn your team as you approach your contract limits, so a renewal
conversation isn't a surprise.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Usage Notifications">
    On **Settings → Notification**, switch on **Usage Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your thresholds">
    Under **Notification Thresholds**, tick the percentages of your contract limit that should
    trigger an alert. You can pick more than one — for example 80% and 100%.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who hears about it">
    **Notify Administrators** sends the alert to everyone with root/administrator access.
    **Additional Email Recipients** takes a comma-separated list of extra addresses, such as your
    finance inbox.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each threshold notifies you **once per contract period** — it won't repeat as usage hovers around
  the same percentage — and the set resets when a new contract period begins. Usage is counted
  across your sub-accounts as well as the parent account. See
  [Billing and usage](/account-configuration/billing-and-usage).
</Note>

## Configure custom notifications (who gets notified)

For the main workflow events — reference completed, all references completed, leads, feedback, pre-screening, and unresponsive — you can choose exactly who receives the email.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the custom notification cards">
    On **Settings → Notification**, scroll to the custom notification cards — one card per event type available on your account (for example, **Reference completed notifications** or **Unresponsive notifications**).

    ![SCREENSHOT: Custom notification cards on the Notification tab showing Reference completed, All references completed, Feedback, Unresponsive — placeholder](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the event you want to configure">
    Click the **Edit** button on the event's card. A modal opens with the recipient options for that event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the recipients">
    Tick one or more recipient options. The options shown vary by event, drawn from:

    | Option | Who receives the email |
    |---|---|
    | **Send to candidate assigned user** | The recruiter assigned to the candidate |
    | **Send to user who added the reference** | The recruiter who originally added that reference |
    | **Send to root email** | The account's root recruiter email |
    | **Send to custom emails** | Any email addresses you type into the custom list (separate multiple addresses with commas) |

    You can enable more than one option at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="For lead notifications, filter by lead type">
    The **Lead notifications** card also lets you filter by lead type (for example, candidate leads vs. hiring leads) if lead types are configured on your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** in the modal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Notification event reference

By default, every custom-notification event goes to the **assigned recruiter only** — enable other recipient options per event if you need them.

| Event | Default recipients |
|---|---|
| Reference completed | Assigned recruiter |
| All references completed | Assigned recruiter |
| Feedback completed | Assigned recruiter |
| Lead submitted | Assigned recruiter |
| Pre-screening completed | Assigned recruiter |
| Unresponsive reference or request | Assigned recruiter |

### When an unresponsive alert fires

The **Unresponsive notifications** card is worth understanding, because the rule behind it is
specific. A reference or reference request is treated as unresponsive when all of the following are
true:

- it still hasn't been filled in;
- its automatic reminders have run out — there's no further reminder scheduled; and
- the last reminder went out more than 24 hours ago.

Vitay then emails the recipients you selected on that card. Two things to know:

- **The alert is sent once per record.** If the same reference goes quiet again later, you won't get
  a second email — check the record itself for its current state.
- **If no recipient option is ticked on the Unresponsive card, no alert is ever sent.** Unlike the
  other events, there's no fallback recipient here.

<Note>
  Separately, a candidate sitting in **Process initiated** is moved to the **Unresponsive** status
  when one of their reference requests goes quiet, and moves back automatically once it's responsive
  again. See
  [Candidate statuses and comments](/candidates-reference-requests/candidate-statuses-and-comments).
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I'm not receiving notifications I expect">
    First check that the relevant toggle is enabled on **Settings → Notification**. Then open the custom notification card for that event and confirm your email (or "assigned user") is selected as a recipient. Also check your spam folder — notification emails from Vitay's shared sender domain can occasionally be filtered.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Too many people are getting notified">
    Open each custom notification card and deselect the recipient options you don't need. For example, if only the assigned recruiter should hear about completions, make sure **Send to root email** and **Send to custom emails** are turned off for that event.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see a card for a particular event">
    Some cards only appear when the matching feature is active on your account (for example, feedback, pre-screening, or leads). Contact **hello@vitay.io** if you believe a feature should be available to your account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
