---
title: "Branding and white label"
description: "Customize your logo, colors, and sender identity so candidate-facing pages and emails reflect your firm's brand."
---

Add your logo and brand colors to what candidates and references see. With the white label plugin, Vitay also runs under your own domain with your colors and font.

<Note>
  You need the root recruiter role. Your logo applies to reports and emails right away. Colors and font apply only with white label on, on your custom domain.
</Note>

## Upload your logo

Your logo appears on candidate reports and on the emails Vitay sends to references, with or without white label.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the General tab">
    In the Configuration area, click **Settings**. The **General** tab opens first.

    ![SCREENSHOT(branding-white-label-general): The General tab in Settings with the Company name, Logo, and Primary color and Secondary color fields](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your logo">
    In the **Logo** card, click the image (it shows **Upload here** on hover) and choose an image file. A PNG or SVG with a transparent background works best. The logo saves as soon as it uploads.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set your brand colors

**Primary color** and **Secondary color** style buttons, tabs and other elements on pages served from your white label domain. On Vitay's own addresses (such as references.vitay.io), Vitay's colors always apply. With white label on, the primary color is also used for buttons in emails.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the General tab">
    In Settings, click the **General** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the colors">
    Click **Primary color** or **Secondary color** and pick a color, or type a hex code such as `#0E7C87`. Each color saves when you close its picker.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Other settings on the General tab

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Company name** | The name shown on your candidate reports. |
| **Nickname** | A short name that tells your sub-accounts apart. Shown only when your account has sub-accounts. |
| **Default country** | The default dialling code for phone number fields. |
| **Default timezone** | The timezone dates and times are shown in. |
| **Default date format** | How dates are written, for example `Jan 01, 2020`. |
| **Default language** | The language of the app and of the emails Vitay sends. Changing it reloads the app. A personal language on your own account page still wins for you. |

The coloured message bar across the top of the app is the **Information banner**, on the **Configuration** tab (see [Firm configuration](/account-configuration/firm-configuration)).

## Set up white label

White label replaces Vitay's branding with your logo, colors and font on the pages your candidates and references see, served from your own domain. It's a plugin Vitay turns on for you. Until then, the **White Label** tab doesn't appear in Settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request the plugin">
    Click **Plugins** in the Configuration navigation and click **Request plugin** on the **White label** card. This opens an email to **hello@vitay.io**. Wait for the Vitay team to confirm it's on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the White Label tab">
    In Settings, click the **White Label** tab. The **Enable / Disable white label** switch is already on.

    ![SCREENSHOT(branding-white-label-tab): The White Label tab with the Enable / Disable white label switch, Font family, Domain name, Explainer video link and User guide link fields](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your domain">
    Type the domain you want to use in **Domain name**, for example `references.yourcompany.com`. It saves when you stop typing. Contact **hello@vitay.io** for the DNS record to add at your domain provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add links for your team's welcome email (optional)">
    Fill in **Explainer video link** and **User guide link**. New recruiters get these links in their welcome email instead of Vitay's.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a font (optional)">
    Pick a typeface in **Font family**. The default is Open Sans.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Turning off **Enable / Disable white label** turns off the plugin, and the **White Label** tab disappears the next time Settings loads. Your saved values are kept, but you need to ask **hello@vitay.io** to turn it back on.
</Warning>

## Remove "powered by Vitay" from emails

Reference emails end with a short note about opening the link and "Reference checking powered by Vitay.io". **Private label** removes that footer. It isn't in Settings; ask **hello@vitay.io** to turn it on. It's usually turned on together with white label.

To control the sender name and address on emails, see [Email sender settings](/account-configuration/email-sender-settings).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My logo isn't showing on reports or emails">
    Check the logo on the **General** tab. If it looks wrong or is missing, upload a fresh copy. A PNG or SVG with a transparent background works best.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Brand colors don't show on candidate pages">
    Colors and font only apply with white label on and on pages served from your white label domain. Pages on Vitay's own addresses use Vitay's colors. Check that **Domain name** is set on the **White Label** tab and that your links use that domain.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see a White Label tab">
    The tab only appears while the white label plugin is on. Request it from **Plugins** in the Configuration navigation, or contact **hello@vitay.io**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Emails still say 'powered by Vitay.io'">
    That footer is removed by **Private label**, which the Vitay team turns on. Write to **hello@vitay.io**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Dates or times look wrong for my team">
    Check **Default timezone** and **Default date format** on the **General** tab. They apply to the whole account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My custom domain isn't working">
    The domain needs a DNS record at your domain provider, and DNS changes can take time to spread. If it still doesn't work, contact **hello@vitay.io** with your domain name.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
