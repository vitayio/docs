---
title: "Branding and white label"
description: "Customize your logo, colors, and sender identity so candidate-facing pages and emails reflect your firm's brand."
---

Vitay lets you apply your firm's visual identity to the emails and pages your candidates and references see. You can upload a logo, set brand colors, and — with the white-label plugin — serve Vitay under your own custom domain.

<Note>
  You need a **root recruiter** role to change branding settings. Your logo applies to reports and outgoing emails right away; in-app colors and fonts apply on your white-label custom domain (see below).
</Note>

## Upload your logo

Your logo appears on candidate reference reports and on the emails Vitay sends to references. It works whether or not white label is enabled.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open General settings">
    Go to **Settings → General**.

    ![Settings → General, with company name and logo](/images/branding-general.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your logo">
    In the **Logo** section, click **Upload here** and select an image file from your computer. We recommend a PNG or SVG with a transparent background.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The logo is saved automatically after uploading. Refresh the page to confirm it appears correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set your brand colors

Primary and secondary colors are used on buttons, tabs, and UI elements on the pages served from your **white-label custom domain**. On the standard `app.vitay.io` address, Vitay's own colors always apply — your logo on reports and emails is unaffected either way.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open General settings">
    Go to **Settings → General**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the primary color">
    In the **Primary color** field, enter your brand color as a hex code (for example, `#0E7C87`) or use the color picker. This color is used for buttons and key interactive elements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the secondary color">
    In the **Secondary color** field, enter your secondary brand color. This is used for supporting UI elements.

    Each color is saved when you close its picker — there is no separate Save button.

    ![SCREENSHOT: The color picker fields for primary and secondary brand colors — placeholder](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Enable white-label mode

White-label mode replaces Vitay's branding with your own logo, colors, and font on the pages your candidates and references see, served from a custom domain name.

<Note>
  White label is a **plugin that Vitay enables for your account on request**. Go to **Settings → Plugins** and use the request option there — it opens an email to **hello@vitay.io** — or write to that address directly. Once the Vitay team has enabled the plugin, the **White Label** tab in Settings becomes available.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request the plugin">
    On **Settings → Plugins**, request the **White Label** plugin (the request emails **hello@vitay.io**). Wait for confirmation from the Vitay team that it has been enabled on your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the White Label tab">
    Go to **Settings → White Label**.

    ![The White Label settings tab](/images/white-label.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn white label on">
    Turn on the **Enable / Disable white label** toggle. It saves as soon as you flip it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your custom domain">
    In the **Domain name** field, enter the domain you want to use (for example, `references.yourcompany.com`) and click the inline **save** icon next to the field. Your candidates and references will then see your branding on pages served from that domain — Vitay will provide the DNS record to add at your domain provider; contact **hello@vitay.io** to arrange it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add custom links for your team's welcome email">
    Optionally update the **Explainer video link** and **User guide link** fields, saving each with its inline **save** icon. New recruiters receive these links in their welcome email, so you can point them to your own onboarding materials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a font (optional)">
    In the **Font family** field, select the typeface used across your white-label pages. The default is Open Sans. The selection saves on change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Email sender branding

Your logo also flows into outgoing emails. To control the sender name and email address references see (separate from visual branding), see [Email sender settings](/account-configuration/email-sender-settings).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My logo isn't appearing on reports or emails">
    Make sure you have uploaded the logo in **Settings → General**. Also check that the image file is not corrupted — try re-uploading a fresh copy. A PNG or SVG with a transparent background works best.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Brand colors are not showing on candidate pages">
    Your colors and font apply only on pages served from your **white-label custom domain**. Pages opened on the standard `app.vitay.io` address always use Vitay's own colors. Confirm that white label is enabled, that a domain is set in **Settings → White Label**, and that your candidates and references are reaching Vitay through that domain.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see a White Label tab">
    White label is a plugin that must be enabled by the Vitay team. Request it from **Settings → Plugins** (the request emails **hello@vitay.io**) or contact **hello@vitay.io** directly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My custom domain isn't working">
    Custom domains need a DNS record at your domain provider, which Vitay provides when setting up your white label. DNS changes can take some time to propagate. If your domain still isn't working, contact **hello@vitay.io** with your domain name so the team can verify the configuration.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
