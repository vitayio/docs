---
title: "Candidate feedback"
description: "Collect feedback directly from a candidate, or from an employer or recruiter, using Vitay's feedback request flow."
---

Feedback requests send a questionnaire about a candidate's placement or experience. The recipient
fills it in online and the answers appear on the candidate's **Feedback** tab. Feedback uses the
same question sets as references.

<Note>
  Vitay turns feedback on for your firm. **Candidate** and **Employer** feedback come together;
  **Recruiter** feedback is turned on separately. Contact [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io)
  if a type is missing. Any recruiter can send feedback requests; creating feedback question sets
  needs the root recruiter or supervisor role.
</Note>

## Feedback types

| Type | Who fills it in | Typical use |
|------|-----------------|------------|
| **Candidate** | The candidate | Candidate experience surveys |
| **Employer** | A hiring manager or client contact | Post-hire check-ins |
| **Recruiter** | The candidate, rating their assigned recruiter | Recruiter service ratings |

Recruiter feedback goes to the candidate, not to the recruiter. When you pick it, the request form
reminds you: *"Candidate will rate the assigned user"*.

## Create a feedback question set

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Question sets">
    Click **Question sets** in the left sidebar, then add a set or open one to edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Type to Feedback">
    In the **Type** dropdown, choose **Feedback**, then save. Only Feedback sets appear in feedback
    requests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your questions">
    Build it like a reference question set. See
    [Question sets and templates](/account-configuration/question-sets-and-templates).
  </Step>
</Steps>

One Feedback set works for all three types; you choose the type when you send the request.

## Send a feedback request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate's Feedback tab">
    Go to **Candidates**, open the candidate, and click the **Feedback** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Feedback request">
    The button is at the top right of the tab.

    ![SCREENSHOT(candidate-feedback-tab): A candidate's Feedback tab with the Feedback request button, the feedback surveys table and the employers table](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the Feedback type">
    - **Candidate**: Vitay sends it to the email on the candidate's profile.
    - **Employer**: enter the employer's first name, last name and email. Add a phone number to
      also send it by text message.
    - **Recruiter**: Vitay sends it to the candidate.

    ![SCREENSHOT(candidate-feedback-request-modal): The Feedback request dialog with the Feedback type options and the Question set dropdown](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Question set">
    Only Feedback question sets are listed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a Scheduled date (optional)">
    If your firm has scheduled feedback, pick a date to send it later, or leave it blank to send
    now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Submit">
    Vitay emails the request, and also texts it when the recipient has a phone number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens after you send

- The recipient gets a link to the feedback form.
- If they don't answer, Vitay sends reminders on your firm's reminder schedule, by text too if they
  have a phone number and your firm uses text messages.
- When they submit, the answers and a score (from rating and matrix questions) appear on the
  candidate's **Feedback** tab.
- An email goes to the people chosen in the **Feedback completed** row of **Team notifications**
  (**Settings → Notifications** in Configuration). For new accounts, that's the candidate's
  assigned recruiter and the person who sent the request.

To stop feedback reminders, a root recruiter can turn off **Feedback reminders** on the same
Notifications tab. Reference reminders aren't affected. See
[Notification settings](/account-configuration/notification-settings).

## The Feedback page

**Feedback** in the left sidebar lists every feedback request across your candidates, with the
question set, type, candidate, score, assigned user, and requested and completed dates. You can
filter by date range and by type, and switch between **Everyone** and **Myself** if your role or
firm settings allow it.

![SCREENSHOT(candidate-feedback-list): The Feedback page listing feedback requests with the date range picker, type filter and Everyone / Myself toggle](/images/placeholder.png)

Click a request to open it. On a pending request you can click **Send reminder** to send a reminder
now.

## Complete a form on someone's behalf

If a candidate or employer gives you their answers by phone, open the pending request (from the
Feedback page, or by clicking its row on the candidate's Feedback tab) and click **Complete
feedback**. The recipient's form opens in a new tab for you to fill in and submit.

<Warning>
  Answers entered this way are saved as if the recipient submitted them, and the report doesn't
  show who typed them. Only use it to record what the person told you.
</Warning>

## Employers

Employer feedback creates an employer record on the candidate. On the candidate's **Feedback** tab,
the **employers** table lists them. Click a row to edit the employer's name, email, phone, the
candidate's position and specialty, and start and end dates.

If your firm has the ATS plugin, an **Employers** page in the left sidebar lists every employer in
the account. Edits still happen on the candidate.

To export feedback answers in bulk, see [Download reports](/reports-lead-generation/download-reports).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A feedback type is missing">
    Candidate and Employer feedback are turned on together, and Recruiter feedback separately.
    Contact [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) to turn on the one you need.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't choose Feedback as a question set Type">
    The option only appears once feedback is turned on for your firm.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The candidate didn't get the feedback email">
    Check the email on the candidate's profile and ask them to look in spam. To send a reminder now,
    open the request from the **Feedback** page and click **Send reminder**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I need to change an employer's email">
    On the candidate's **Feedback** tab, click the employer in the **employers** table and correct
    it. The next reminder goes to the new address.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
