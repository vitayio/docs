---
title: "Correcting a reference's email address"
description: "For candidates: how to fix or update the email address of a reference you listed on your reference request."
---

<Note>
  **This article is for candidates** who listed their references on a reference request. If you were asked to *give* a reference and your own email address has changed, simply reply to the email you received and let the recruiter know.
</Note>

If you entered the wrong email address for one of your references — or their email has changed since you submitted — there's no self-serve way to correct it. Once your reference request is submitted, the list is locked, and reference details are managed by the recruiter in Vitay.

The good news is that the recruiter can update the address and resend the request in seconds.

## What to do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the recruiter's contact details">
    Open the original reference request email you received from Vitay. The recruiter's name appears in the message, and replying to that email will reach them directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the correct email address">
    Tell the recruiter which reference needs updating and provide the correct email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The recruiter updates and resends">
    Once they make the change, Vitay sends a fresh email to the corrected address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If your reference says they never got the email, it may be a delivery issue rather than a wrong address — see [My reference isn't getting the email](/reference-providers/reference-not-receiving-email) for steps to check first.
</Note>
