---
title: "Correcting a reference's email address"
description: "For candidates: how to fix or update the email address of a reference you listed on your reference request."
---

<Note>
  This article is for candidates who listed their references on a reference request. If you were asked to *give* a reference and your own email address has changed, reply to the email you received and tell the recruiter.
</Note>

After you submit your reference request, you can't edit the list yourself. If you typed a reference's email address wrong, or it has changed, the recruiter can correct it and send the request again.

## What to do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reply to the reference request email">
    Open the email that asked you for your references and reply to it. Replies go to the recruiting team that sent the request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the correct address">
    Say which reference needs updating and give the correct email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The recruiter sends a new email">
    Once they update the address, your reference gets a new request at the corrected address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The address was right but my reference never got the email">
    That's usually a delivery problem rather than a wrong address. See [My reference isn't getting the email](/reference-providers/reference-not-receiving-email) for what to check first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
