---

<Note>
  This article is for candidates who received a reference request. If you were asked to *give* a reference and are stuck, see [Editing your answers after submitting](/reference-providers/edit-my-answers), which covers problems on the reference form.
</Note>

Most problems come from a required field that's still empty, a missing checkbox or signature, or a browser add-on getting in the way.

## Check what the form says is missing

While something is missing, the **Submit** button is red with a cross. Click it anyway: nothing is sent, and a box headed **Please complete the following before submitting:** lists what's left. Items drop off the list as you fix them.

![SCREENSHOT(trouble-submitting-missing-fields): The red Submit button with the "Please complete the following before submitting" list below it on the reference request form](/images/placeholder.png)

What usually shows up there:

- **A field on one of your references**, for example *Ref #2: Email required*. Each reference needs a first name, last name, company name and relationship (tick **N/A** under company or relationship if it doesn't apply), plus a way to reach them. Unless the recruiter asks for something specific, an email address or a mobile number is enough. If the form shows **Start date** and **End date**, those are needed too, or tick **Present**.
- **The terms checkbox**: *Please accept our Terms of Service and acknowledge our Privacy Policy*. Tick **I accept the Terms of Service and acknowledge the Privacy Policy** above **Submit**.
- **An authorization checkbox**: *Please review and accept all the terms above*. Some recruiting teams add their own consent text with a checkbox.
- **Signature**: *Signature is required*. Use the **Draw** tab to sign with your mouse or finger, or the **Type** tab to type your name. **Clear** starts again.
- **At least one document**: the recruiter has made uploads required. Attach a file in the **Documents** section.

## Check the email addresses

- If the form says personal email addresses are not permitted, use a work address for each reference. Addresses at Gmail, Yahoo, Hotmail, AOL and similar are rejected, and the field shows *(personal emails not allowed)*.
- *Reference already attached to your profile* means that person is already on your list. Use someone else.

## If typing or selecting doesn't work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Refresh the page">
    Reload the form. Your entries are saved in your browser as you type, so they should still be there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn off ad-blockers and extensions">
    Ad-blockers and privacy extensions can stop parts of the form from responding. Turn them off for this page, or open the link in a private or incognito window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try another browser or your phone">
    Open the link in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge, or on your phone. If your work network blocks outside links, use mobile data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If you get an error when you click Submit

- **Something went wrong while submitting.** The connection dropped before the form reached Vitay. Your entries are still in your browser. Wait a moment and click **Submit** again.
- **… of … reference(s) failed to submit.** The reason is shown on the reference that failed, for example an invalid email address. Nothing is saved until every reference is accepted, so fix it and submit again. You won't create duplicates.
- **Reference request already filled.** Your submission already went through. To change something, see [Adding another reference after submitting](/reference-providers/add-another-reference).

## Still stuck?

Reply to the email that asked you for your references. Replies go to the recruiting team that sent it. Say what you see on the form (a screenshot helps) so they can check the request or send you a new link.
