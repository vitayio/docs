---
title: "Can I get a copy of what I submitted?"
description: "Whether candidates and people giving a reference can download or receive a copy of what they submitted on a Vitay form, and who to ask."
---

<Note>
  **This article applies to both candidates and people asked to give a reference.** Neither form has a Vitay login, and the answer is the same for both.
</Note>

There is no download, print, or "email me a copy" option on the Vitay forms. Once you click **Submit**, the form shows a confirmation page and your answers go straight to the recruiting team — the form itself doesn't keep a copy for you to open later.

If you'd like a record of what you submitted, ask the recruiter who sent you the request. They can see everything you entered and can decide whether to share it with you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the recruiter's contact details">
    Open the original email you received from Vitay. The recruiter's name is in the message, and replying to that email reaches them directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for a copy">
    Say which form you filled in (your list of references, or the reference you gave for a named candidate) and that you'd like a copy of your submission.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you gave a reference, whether your answers can be shared with the candidate is up to the recruiting team — and, if the form asked you for permission to release your answers to the candidate, on the answer you gave there.
</Note>
