---
title: "Can I get a copy of what I submitted?"
description: "Whether candidates and people giving a reference can download or receive a copy of what they submitted on a Vitay form, and who to ask."
---

<Note>
  This article is for both candidates and people asked to give a reference. The answer is the same for both forms.
</Note>

The forms have no download, print, or "email me a copy" option, and Vitay doesn't send you a copy after you submit. Once you click **Submit**, you see a thank-you page and your answers go to the recruiting team. If you want a record, ask the recruiter.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reply to the email you received">
    Open the email that sent you the form and reply to it. Replies go to the recruiting team that sent it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for a copy">
    Say which form you filled in (your list of references, or the reference you gave for a named candidate) and that you'd like a copy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Whether they share it is up to the recruiting team. If you gave a reference and the form asked **Do we have your permission to release this information to the candidate?**, they'll also take your answer to that into account.
