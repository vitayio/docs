---
title: "I don't recognize this reference request"
description: "What to do if you received a Vitay reference request you weren't expecting or don't recognize."
---

Getting an unexpected reference request can feel alarming, but it's usually straightforward to explain: a job candidate listed you as one of their references and gave your contact details to their recruiter. You may not have been told in advance.

You are not obligated to complete the reference, and there's nothing you need to do to "refuse" formally — you can simply not fill it in.

## Your options

**Not sure if you know the candidate?**

The request email names the candidate you're being asked to reference. If you recognize the name, the request is legitimate. If you genuinely don't know the person, use one of the options below.

**Option 1 — Reply to the request email**

This works in every case: reply to the request email and let the recruiter know you don't recognize the candidate or didn't agree to be listed. The request typically comes from the recruiter who sent it, so replying is the fastest way to reach them. They'll follow up with the candidate to sort it out.

**Option 2 — Decline on the form, if it offers that**

Some companies enable a decline option on the reference form. If yours does, you'll see a link on the form to click if you don't wish to (or are unable to) complete the reference — it asks for a brief reason and notifies the recruiter automatically. If you don't see it, use Option 1 instead.

**Option 3 — Do nothing**

There's no penalty for not completing a form you didn't agree to. Be aware, though, that Vitay sends automated reminder emails for a while after the initial request, so replying to the recruiter (Option 1) is the quickest way to stop them.

<Note>
  If you believe someone used your email address fraudulently, reply to the request email and tell the recruiter immediately. They can remove your details and investigate.
</Note>
