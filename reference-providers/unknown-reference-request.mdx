description: "For people asked to give a reference: what to do if you received a Vitay email you weren't expecting or don't recognize."
---

<Note>
  This article is for people who were asked to give a reference. If you're a job candidate and got a reference request you weren't expecting, reply to the email and ask the recruiter to confirm it's genuine.
</Note>

You got this email because a job candidate listed you as a reference and gave the recruiter your contact details. They may not have told you first. The email names the candidate. You don't have to fill in the form.

## Your options

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the candidate's name">
    If you know the candidate, the request is genuine and you can fill in the form or decline it. If you don't know them, use one of the options below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decline on the form, if offered">
    Open the link. Some recruiting teams add a line near the top of the form: *If this request is not relevant to you or you wish to decline it, please click here.* Click it, leave a short comment saying why, and click **Decline**. The recruiter is notified. If you don't see that line, reply to the email instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or reply to the email">
    Replies go to the recruiting team that sent the request. Tell them you don't recognize the candidate or didn't agree to be listed, and they'll follow up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you do nothing, you may get reminder emails. There's no penalty for not completing a form you didn't agree to.

![SCREENSHOT(unknown-reference-request-decline-link): The top of the reference form with the "If this request is not relevant to you or you wish to decline it, please click here." line](/images/placeholder.png)

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I think someone used my email address without permission">
    Reply to the email and tell the recruiter. They can remove your details and look into it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
