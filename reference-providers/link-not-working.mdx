<Note>
  **This article applies to both candidates and people asked to give a reference.** Whichever form you were sent, the fixes are the same, and only the recruiter can send you a fresh link.
</Note>

If clicking the link in your Vitay email leads to an error — or the ID you were given isn't being accepted — here's what's likely happening and how to fix it.

## Common reasons a link stops working

**The form has already been submitted**

Once a form is submitted, the link is locked. If you see a message saying the reference request or reference is already filled, your submission went through successfully and there's nothing else to do. If you're a candidate and need to change your list of references after this point, see [Adding another reference after submitting](/reference-providers/add-another-reference).

**The request is no longer active**

The recruiter may have closed or removed the request. If so, only the recruiter can reissue it.

**The link was copied incorrectly**

If you copied the link manually from the email (rather than clicking it), check that the full URL was included with no missing characters or extra spaces at the end.

**Your work network is blocking the link**

Some company networks block external links in emails. Try opening the link on your personal device or using your phone's mobile data instead.

## What to do if none of the above helps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the recruiter's contact details">
    Open your original Vitay email. The recruiter's name is mentioned in the message, and replying to that email will reach them directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask them to reissue the link">
    Let the recruiter know the link isn't working. They can check the status and send you a fresh email if needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only the recruiter can reissue a link. There is no self-serve way to generate a new one.
</Note>
