<Note>
  This article is for both candidates and people asked to give a reference. The fixes are the same for both forms, and only the recruiter can send you a new link.
</Note>

These are the usual reasons a link from a Vitay email shows an error or doesn't load.

## Common reasons

### The form was already submitted

A submitted form can't be opened again. You'll see **Reference request already filled** (candidates) or **Reference has already been filled** (references). Your submission went through and there's nothing more to do. Candidates who need to change their list can see [Adding another reference after submitting](/reference-providers/add-another-reference).

### The request was removed

If the recruiter deleted the request, the page says so, for example **Reference deleted**. Only the recruiter can send a new one.

### The link was copied incompletely

If you copied the link instead of clicking it, check that you have the whole address with no missing characters or extra spaces.

### Your work network blocks it

Some company networks block outside links. Open the link on a personal device or on your phone's mobile data.

## If none of these help

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reply to the email you received">
    Replies go to the recruiting team that sent the request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for a new link">
    Say the link isn't working and what the page shows. The recruiter can check the request and send you a new email.
  </Step>
</Steps>
