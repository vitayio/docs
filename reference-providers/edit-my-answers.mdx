---
title: "Editing your answers after submitting"
description: "For people giving a reference: whether you can change your answers once you've submitted the reference form, and what to do if you had trouble submitting."
---

<Note>
  This article is for people who were asked to give a reference. If you're the candidate and want to change the reference details you submitted, see [Correcting a reference's email address](/reference-providers/change-reference-email) or [Adding another reference after submitting](/reference-providers/add-another-reference).
</Note>

When you click **Submit**, the form asks you to confirm because you can't change your answers afterwards. If you open the link again, you'll see **Reference has already been filled**. The recruiter can correct an answer on their side.

## I submitted but need to change something

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reply to the email you received">
    Open the email that asked you to give a reference and reply to it. Replies go to the recruiting team that sent it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what needs to change">
    Tell them which question you want to correct and what the answer should be.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## I'm having trouble submitting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Submit button is red and nothing is sent">
    Something required is still empty. Click **Submit** and a list headed **Please complete the following before submitting:** shows what's missing. Required questions are marked, and some ratings need a comment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I get an error when I click Submit">
    Refresh the page and try again. Your answers are saved in your browser as you type, so they should still be there. If the error says the reference has already been filled, your submission went through and there's nothing more to do.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The page keeps failing to load or freezing">
    Try another browser, such as Google Chrome, or your phone on mobile data instead of a work network. Some company networks block outside links.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="None of this worked">
    Reply to the email you received and describe what you see. The recruiter can check the request or send you a new link.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
