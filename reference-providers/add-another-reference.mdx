---
title: "Adding another reference after submitting"
description: "For candidates: how to add another reference once you've already submitted your list of references."
---

<Note>
  **This article is for candidates** who received a reference request and have already submitted their references. If you were asked to *give* a reference, see [Help for candidates and references](/reference-providers/start-here).
</Note>

Thank you for submitting your references. Once you click **Submit** on the reference request form, your list is locked — if you open the link again, you'll see a **Reference request already filled** message. There's no self-serve way to add another reference from your end.

If the recruiter has asked you for an additional reference, or you realize you left someone out, the recruiter can add them for you.

## What to do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the recruiter's contact details">
    Open the original reference request email you received from Vitay. The recruiter's name is mentioned in the email, and you can reply directly to reach them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send them the new reference's details">
    Tell the recruiter you'd like to add another reference. Include the person's name, email address, and how you know them (for example, "my manager at Acme from 2022 to 2024").
  </Step>
  <Step title="The recruiter adds the reference">
    Once the recruiter adds them to your list, Vitay sends the new reference their own email automatically. It's worth giving your reference a heads-up so they know to expect it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you can't find the original email, check your spam or junk folder — it was sent from a Vitay-powered address on behalf of the recruiter.
</Note>
