---
title: "Adding another reference after submitting"
description: "For candidates: how to add another reference once you've already submitted your list of references."
---

<Note>
  This article is for candidates who received a reference request and have already submitted their references. If you were asked to *give* a reference, see [Help for candidates and references](/reference-providers/start-here).
</Note>

Once you click **Submit** on the reference request form, your list is locked. If you open the link again, you'll see **Reference request already filled**. To add someone now, ask the recruiter to add them for you.

## What to do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reply to the reference request email">
    Open the email that asked you for your references and reply to it. Replies go to the recruiting team that sent the request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the new reference's details">
    Include the person's name, email address or phone number, and how you know them (for example, "my manager at Acme from 2022 to 2024").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let your reference know">
    Once the recruiter adds them, Vitay sends your reference their own request. Tell them to expect it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you haven't submitted yet, you may be able to add more people yourself: look for the **Add another reference** button below the last reference on the form. It only appears if the recruiter allows extra references.

![SCREENSHOT(add-another-reference-button): The Add another reference button below the reference cards on the reference request form](/images/placeholder.png)

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't find the original email">
    Check your spam or junk folder. The sender name usually reads "Recruiter name via Company name".
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
