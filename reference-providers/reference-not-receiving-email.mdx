---
title: "My reference isn't getting the email"
description: "For candidates: what to do if someone you listed as a reference says they haven't received the Vitay email."
---

<Note>
  This article is for candidates who listed their references on a reference request. If you were asked to *give* a reference and can't find the email, check your spam or junk folder, then ask the candidate or the recruiter to have it sent again.
</Note>

If someone you listed says the email never arrived, check these things before asking the recruiter to resend it. Only the recruiter can resend it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask them to check spam or junk">
    Ask your reference to look in their **Spam**, **Junk**, or **Promotions** folder. Company email filters sometimes hold these messages. The sender name usually reads "Recruiter name via Company name".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the address you entered">
    One typo, like `.con` instead of `.com`, sends the email somewhere else. If the address is wrong, see [Correcting a reference's email address](/reference-providers/change-reference-email).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask the recruiter to resend">
    Reply to the email that asked you for your references and ask the recruiter to resend it. Mention that your reference already checked spam and that the address is correct. If their work inbox blocks outside emails, give the recruiter another address for them.
  </Step>
</Steps>
