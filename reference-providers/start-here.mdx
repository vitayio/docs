<Note>
  **Are you a recruiter or hiring manager using Vitay?** This section is for people who were asked to *give* a reference — it won't cover the recruiter app. Head to the [recruiter docs](/candidates-reference-requests/overview) instead.
</Note>

Welcome, and thank you for taking the time to complete a reference. Someone listed you as a reference for a job opportunity, and the company's recruiting team uses Vitay to collect that information securely online.

You don't need a Vitay account. Everything you need is in the reference request email you received, including the link to your form.

**If you have questions about the request itself** — the candidate, the role, or the hiring process — reply to your request email. The request typically comes from the recruiter who sent it, so replying is the fastest way to reach them (exact sender details depend on the company's email settings). For technical problems with the form itself, you can email Vitay support at hello@vitay.io.
