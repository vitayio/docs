<Note>
  Are you a recruiter or hiring manager using Vitay? This section is for people outside the recruiting team. Go to the [recruiter docs](/candidates-reference-requests/overview) instead.
</Note>

Recruiting teams use Vitay to collect job references online. Two kinds of people get messages from Vitay:

- The candidate: you applied for a role and got a reference request asking for the names and contact details of your references.
- The reference: a candidate listed you, and you got a link to a form about your experience working with them.

You don't need a Vitay account either way. The email (or text message) you received has the link to your form. If something isn't working, reply to the email: replies go to the recruiting team that sent it.
