<Note>
  **Are you a recruiter or hiring manager using Vitay?** This section is for people outside the recruiting team who received a Vitay email — it won't cover the recruiter app. Head to the [recruiter docs](/candidates-reference-requests/overview) instead.
</Note>

A company's recruiting team uses Vitay to collect references securely online. That means two different people can receive an email from Vitay, and the help you need depends on which one you are:

- **You're the candidate.** You applied for a role and received a **reference request** asking you to provide the names and contact details of your references.
- **You were asked to give a reference.** A candidate listed you as a reference, and you received a link to a form asking about your experience working with them.

You don't need a Vitay account in either case. Everything you need is in the email you received — including a link to the form and the name of the recruiter who sent it.

**If something isn't working**, the quickest path forward is to reply to your email or contact the recruiter directly. Their name and email are in the message Vitay sent you.
