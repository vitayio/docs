---
title: "Search for past references"
description: "Find references already collected on a candidate."
---

Vitay keeps every completed reference, so you can look up a candidate's past references before sending a new request.

<Note>
  By default you see your own candidates and references. Root and supervisor
  users always see the whole firm's. Other recruiters can switch to
  **Everyone** only when the firm has turned on **Display Everyone / Myself
  Toggle**.
</Note>

## Search from the top bar

Click the magnifier (**Search**) in the top bar to open a search box, then start typing. Results appear as you type. The dropdown beside the box sets what you search:

- **Search candidates** opens the candidate's page.
- **Search references** opens the reference.

Vitay switches it to candidates on the Candidates pages and to references on the References page; you can change it at any time. The top-bar search isn't available on demo accounts.

![SCREENSHOT(search-past-references-topbar-search): The top bar with the search box open and the Search candidates / Search references dropdown](/images/placeholder.png)

## Find references on a candidate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the candidate">
    Go to **Candidates** and type a name, email or internal ID into the
    **Search candidates** box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the References tab">
    The candidate page opens on **References**, which lists every reference on
    the candidate, grouped by question set.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a reference">
    Click a completed reference to read the answers. See
    [Read a completed reference](/candidates-reference-requests/read-a-completed-reference).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Filter the Candidates list

The toolbar starts compact: refresh, **Filters** (funnel), import, add, a date range, the **Search candidates** box, and the **Everyone** / **Myself** switch if you have it. Click the funnel to show the filters:

| Filter | What it does |
|---|---|
| Date range | Presets **Last 7 days**, **Month to date**, **Last month**, **Year to date**, or your own dates. |
| **Search job title...** | Shown only when job titles are on for your firm. |
| **Statuses** | Two groups in one dropdown: **Statuses** (the candidate status) and **Reference status** (**No Reference req. sent**, **Candidate req. pending**, **Reference req. pending**, **Completed**, **Unresponsive**). |
| **Recruiters** | Candidates assigned to the recruiters you tick. |

Tick boxes in a dropdown and click **Apply filters**. The red **✕** at the end clears every filter, switches back to **Myself** and collapses the toolbar.

The date range starts on your firm's default timeframe, not empty. If an older candidate seems to be missing, widen the range.

When a list has more than five pages, a **Page [ ] of N** box appears beside the page numbers. Type a number and press **Enter** to jump to that page.

![SCREENSHOT(search-past-references-candidates-filters): The Candidates list with the filter toolbar expanded, showing the date range, Statuses and Recruiters dropdowns and the red reset button](/images/placeholder.png)

## Find references across all candidates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open References">
    In the left sidebar, click **References**. The list shows the newest
    references first and starts on **Myself**. Switch to **Everyone** to see
    the firm's references, if you have the switch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Type a name or email into the **Search references** box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter">
    Click the funnel to show the filters:

    | Filter | What it does |
    |---|---|
    | Date range | Same presets as the Candidates list. |
    | **Recruiters** | References belonging to the recruiters you tick. |
    | **Question sets** | References sent with the sets you tick. The statistics above the list and the CSV download follow it. |
    | **Types** | **Client**, **Candidate**, **Duplicated IP** or **N/A**. **Duplicated IP** finds references that share an IP address with their candidate. |
    | **Relationships** | The reference's relationship to the candidate. |
    | **Status** | **Pending**, **Completed** or **Unresponsive**. |

    Each dropdown has **Apply filters**. The red **✕** clears them all and
    returns to **Myself**.

    ![SCREENSHOT(search-past-references-references-filters): The References list with the filter toolbar expanded, showing Recruiters, Question sets, Types, Relationships and Status](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a reference">
    Click a row to read the reference.
  </Step>
</Steps>

With the filters open, root and supervisor users (and everyone, if your firm allows report downloads) also get a download button (**Download CSV**) that exports the filtered list. See [Download reports](/reports-lead-generation/download-reports).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't find a candidate I know exists">
    Widen the date range first. If the candidate was deactivated, they're in
    the **Recycle Bin** in the left sidebar, where you can reactivate them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The References tab is empty but a request was sent">
    References appear once the candidate submits their contacts or you add one
    yourself. Until then, the open request shows as a banner at the top of the
    candidate page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
