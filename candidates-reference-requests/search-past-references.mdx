---
title: "Search for past references"
description: "Find references already collected on a candidate."
---

Vitay stores every reference that has been completed, even after a hire is made. If a candidate has been through your process before, you may already have references on file that you can review without sending a new request.

<Note>
  You need a recruiter (or higher) role to view references. By default you see
  your own references; root recruiters and supervisors always see the whole
  firm's. Other recruiters can browse firm-wide only when your firm has opted
  in by enabling the **Display Everyone / Myself toggle** setting.
</Note>

## Search from anywhere with the top bar

The fastest way to jump to a specific person is the **magnifier** icon in the top bar. Click it to
open a search box, then start typing — results appear as you type.

The search box has a dropdown next to it that decides what you're searching:

- **Search candidates** — jumps to the candidate's profile.
- **Search references** — jumps to the reference's page.

Vitay switches the dropdown for you as you move around: it searches candidates while you're on the
Candidates pages and references while you're on the References pages, and you can change it
manually at any time.

<Note>
  The top-bar search is hidden on demo accounts, and it's not shown while you have a single
  candidate open.
</Note>

## Find references on a candidate record

The quickest way to see past references for a specific candidate is through their profile in Vitay.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate">
    Go to **Candidates** and search for the candidate by name, email, or internal ID.

    ![The Candidates list with the search bar](/images/candidates-list.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the References tab">
    On the candidate page, click the **References** tab. This lists every
    reference collected for this candidate — completed, pending, and manually
    added.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the reference">
    Click a completed reference to open it and read the answers and scores.
    See [Read a completed reference](/candidates-reference-requests/read-a-completed-reference)
    for a full walkthrough of the reference view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Narrow the Candidates list

The Candidates list starts with a compact toolbar: a **refresh** icon, a **Filters** (funnel) icon,
a date-range picker and a **Search candidates** box. Click the **Filters** funnel to expand the
full filter toolbar.

| Filter | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Date range** | Limits the list by date. Presets are **Last 7 days**, **Month to date**, **Last month** and **Year to date**, or pick your own start and end dates. |
| **Search job title…** | Searches candidates by job title. Only shown when job titles are enabled for your firm. |
| **Statuses** | One dropdown with two sections. **Statuses** filters on the candidate status — Process initiated, Waitlisted, Interview scheduled, Rejected, Interviewed, Completed, Submitted, Accepted, Unresponsive. **Reference status** filters on where their references are — No Reference req. sent, Candidate req. pending, Reference req. pending, Completed, Unresponsive. |
| **Recruiters** | Limits the list to candidates assigned to the recruiters you tick. |
| **Everyone / Myself** | Switches between your own candidates and the whole firm's. |

Tick the boxes you want inside a dropdown and click **Apply filters**. To clear everything, click
the red **✕** at the end of the toolbar — it resets every filter, switches the view back to
**Myself**, and collapses the toolbar again.

<Tip>
  The date range doesn't start empty. When you open the list, Vitay pre-selects the default
  timeframe your firm has configured, so widen the range if an older candidate seems to be
  missing.
</Tip>

## Find references across all candidates

If you're not sure which candidate a reference is tied to, or you want to browse recently completed references across your book of business, use the global references view.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open References in the sidebar">
    In the left sidebar, click **References**. This shows a paginated list of
    references, most recent first. The list defaults to **Myself** — your own
    references. If your firm has enabled the **Display Everyone / Myself
    toggle** setting (or you're a root recruiter or supervisor), you can
    switch the toggle to **Everyone** to browse the whole firm's references.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search by name or email">
    Use the **Search references** box in the toolbar to find a reference by
    their name or email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the list">
    Click the **Filters** (funnel) icon to expand the filter toolbar:

    | Filter | What it does |
    |---|---|
    | **Date range** | Same presets as elsewhere — **Last 7 days**, **Month to date**, **Last month**, **Year to date**, or custom dates. |
    | **Recruiters** | Limits the list to references belonging to the recruiters you tick. |
    | **Types** | **Client**, **Candidate**, **Duplicated IP** and **N/A**. The Client and Candidate types come from lead generation; **Duplicated IP** picks out references flagged for sharing an IP address with their candidate. |
    | **Relationships** | Filters by the reference's relationship to the candidate, using the relationship list your firm has configured. |
    | **Status** | **Pending**, **Completed** or **Unresponsive**. |

    Each dropdown has an **Apply filters** button. The red **✕** at the end of
    the toolbar clears all of them and returns the view to **Myself**.

    ![SCREENSHOT: the References list with the filter toolbar expanded showing Recruiters, Types, Relationships and Status — placeholder](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a reference">
    Click any row to open the individual reference and read its answers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  With the filter toolbar open you may also see a **download** icon, which exports the filtered
  list as a CSV file. It appears for root recruiters, and for everyone else when your firm allows
  report downloads. See [Download reports](/reports-lead-generation/download-reports).
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't find a candidate I know exists">
    By default the Candidates list shows only active candidates. If the
    candidate was deactivated, open the **Recycle Bin** in the left sidebar —
    deactivated candidates live there, and you can reactivate them yourself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The References tab is empty even though references were sent">
    References only appear here once they've been added (either by the
    candidate entering a reference's details, or by you adding one manually).
    If the request was sent but the candidate hasn't submitted any references
    yet, the tab will be empty. Look for the open-request banner at the top of
    the candidate page instead to see the pending request status.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
