Background screening lets you order a background check on a candidate without
leaving Vitay. The check itself is run by a screening partner — **Mintz Global
Screening** or **Validity Screening Solutions** — which contacts the candidate
directly to collect what it needs. Vitay shows you the status of each check and
lets you download the partner's report when it's complete.

<Note>
  Background screening is activated by the Vitay team, per partner:

  - **Mintz Global Screening** (Canada) — the **Background screening** plugin.
    A root recruiter can request it from **Configuration → Plugins**.
  - **Validity Screening Solutions** (United States) — a Vitay-managed plugin
    with no marketplace card. Email **hello@vitay.io** to ask for it.

  The Vitay team also sets up which screening products (categories) your
  account can order. Any recruiter with access to the candidate can then send
  a request. Each completed check is billed by Vitay — ask your account
  manager for pricing.
</Note>

## Request a background check

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate's Background screening tab">
    Open the candidate and click the **Background screening** tab. The tab is
    only shown when at least one partner is active on your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Background screening request">
    Click **Background screening request**. The **Background screening
    requests** modal opens (*Send a background screening request to your
    candidate*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the partner, if you have both">
    When both partners are active, the modal shows two tabs: **Mintz** and
    **Validity Screening**. With a single partner there are no tabs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a category and submit">
    Under **Select background screening category**, pick one product. Some
    products show a **?** icon — hover it for the product's description. Click
    **Submit**. A **Background screening requested** toast confirms the order
    was placed.

    ![SCREENSHOT: the Background screening requests modal with the Mintz / Validity Screening tabs and the category radio list — placeholder](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

The candidate must have an **email address** on their profile: Vitay passes
their name and email (and, for Mintz, their phone number) to the partner, which
invites the candidate to complete its own application. Vitay itself does not
email the candidate about background checks.

## Follow the status

The Background screening tab lists every check for the candidate with
**Type** (the product), **Status**, **Notes**, **Created at**, **Filled on** and
**Download**.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Process initiated** | The order was placed with the partner. |
| **In progress** | Mintz is working on the case. |
| **Invitation sent** / **Invitation accepted** | Validity has emailed the candidate / the candidate opened the invitation. |
| **Application begun** / **Application completed** | The candidate is filling in / has finished Validity's application. |
| **Screening begun** | Validity has started the search. |
| **Completed (Clear)** | The search found nothing to report. |
| **Completed (Not Clear)** | The partner flagged a possible record — open the report and review it. |

Statuses update when the partner notifies Vitay. To pull the latest status on
demand, click the **refresh** button next to **Background screening request**;
Vitay re-queries the partner for every check on the tab and shows **Background
checkings refreshed**.

When a product is a bundle of several searches, the row expands into a sub-table
listing each search with its **ID**, **Status**, **Last Updated** and its own
**Download** button.

![SCREENSHOT: the Background screening tab with one Completed (Clear) row and one in-progress row — placeholder](/images/placeholder.png)

## Who gets emailed

Background screening does not use your account's notification settings, and no
reminders are sent to the candidate.

- **When you order** — no email from Vitay. The partner contacts the candidate.
- **While the check progresses** — status-update emails go to the Vitay support
  team (hello@vitay.io), not to your recruiters.
- **When the check completes** — the candidate's **assigned recruiter** receives
  **Background check results for *candidate*** with a **Read *candidate*
  background check results** button that opens the Background screening tab.
  If the assigned recruiter has been deactivated, no one is emailed — check the
  tab instead.

## Download the report

Once a check reaches a **Completed** status, an eye button (**View document**)
appears in the **Download** column; before that the column shows **-**. Click
it to download the partner's PDF report, named
`<candidate>_background_checking_<category>_<id>.pdf`. For bundled products,
download each search's report from its sub-row.

If Vitay can't fetch the file yet you'll see **Document not found — The
requested document could not be found. It may not be ready yet.** Partners
sometimes mark a case complete before the PDF is generated; wait a little, click
refresh, and try again.

## Duplicate requests

- **Validity Screening** — Vitay refuses a second request for the **same
  candidate and category** while an earlier one is still in progress. The modal
  shows an error and no new order is placed. Wait for the open check to
  complete, or pick a different category.
- **Mintz** — requests are not de-duplicated. Submitting the same category
  twice creates two separate orders, so check the tab before re-ordering.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="There's no Background screening tab on the candidate">
    Neither partner is active on your account. A root recruiter can request the
    **Background screening** plugin from **Configuration → Plugins**, or email
    **hello@vitay.io**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The modal shows no categories to pick">
    Your account has no screening products assigned yet. Contact
    **hello@vitay.io** to have them set up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I submitted and got an error">
    For Validity, the most common cause is an open check for the same candidate
    and category (see above). Also confirm the candidate has an email address.
    If it keeps failing, contact **hello@vitay.io** with the candidate's name.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The status hasn't changed in days">
    Click the **refresh** button on the tab to re-query the partner. If the
    candidate hasn't responded to the partner's invitation, ask them to check
    their inbox and spam folder for an email from Mintz or Validity — Vitay
    can't resend it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The download button is missing or the file won't open">
    The button only appears once the check is completed. If you get the
    "may not be ready yet" message, wait and retry. Reports are fetched live
    from the partner, so a partner outage also blocks downloads temporarily.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
