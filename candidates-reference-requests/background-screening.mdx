Background screening lets you order a background check on a candidate from inside Vitay. A screening partner, Mintz Global Screening or Validity Screening Solutions, runs the check and contacts the candidate directly. Vitay shows you each check's status and lets you download the partner's report when it's done.

<Note>
  The Vitay team activates background screening per partner. For Mintz Global
  Screening (Canada), a root recruiter can request the **Background
  screening** plugin from **Configuration → Plugins**. For Validity Screening
  Solutions (United States), email hello@vitay.io. The Vitay team also sets
  which screening products your account can order. After that, any recruiter
  with access to the candidate can send a request. Each completed check is
  billed; ask your account manager for pricing.
</Note>

## Request a background check

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate's Background Screening tab">
    Open the candidate and click the **Background Screening** tab. It only
    appears when at least one partner is active on your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Background screening request">
    The **Background Screening Requests** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the partner, if you have both">
    With both partners active, pick the **Mintz** or **Validity Screening**
    tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a category and submit">
    Under **Select background screening category**, pick one product. Hover
    the **?** icon next to a product to read its description. Click
    **Submit**. A **Background screening requested** message confirms the
    order.

    ![SCREENSHOT(background-screening-request-modal): The Background Screening Requests dialog with the partner tabs and the list of screening categories](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

The candidate needs an email address on their profile. Vitay sends the partner the candidate's name and email (and, for Mintz, their phone number), and the partner invites the candidate to complete its application. Vitay doesn't email the candidate about background checks.

## Follow the status

The **Background Screening** tab lists every check for the candidate with **Type** (the product), **Status**, **Notes**, **Created at**, **Filled on** and **Download**.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Process initiated** | The order was placed with the partner. |
| **In progress** | Mintz is working on the case. |
| **Candidate submitted** | The candidate finished Mintz's application; the search hasn't started yet. |
| **On hold (information needed)** | Mintz is waiting for information or a document, usually from the candidate. |
| **Cancelled** | The case was cancelled at Mintz. You can send a new request. |
| **Invitation sent** / **Invitation accepted** | Validity emailed the candidate / the candidate opened the invitation. |
| **Application begun** / **Application completed** | The candidate is filling in / has finished Validity's application. |
| **Screening begun** | Validity has started the search. |
| **Completed** | The check is finished. |
| **Completed (Clear)** | The search found nothing to report. |
| **Completed (Not Clear)** | The partner flagged a possible record. Review the report. |

Statuses update when the partner notifies Vitay. To get the latest status now, click the refresh button next to **Background screening request**. A **Background checkings refreshed** message confirms it.

When a product bundles several searches, the row expands to list each search with its own status and download button.

![SCREENSHOT(background-screening-tab): The candidate's Background Screening tab with a completed check and one in progress](/images/placeholder.png)

## Who gets emailed

Background screening doesn't use your account's notification settings, and Vitay sends the candidate no reminders.

- **When you order:** Vitay sends no email. The partner contacts the candidate.
- **While the check progresses:** status updates go to the Vitay support team, not to your recruiters.
- **When the check completes:** the candidate's assigned recruiter gets an email titled *Background check results for &lt;candidate&gt;*, with a button that opens the Background Screening tab. If the assigned recruiter has been deactivated, nobody is emailed, so check the tab.

## Download the report

Once a check is completed, an eye button (**View document**) appears in the **Download** column. Click it to download the partner's PDF report. For bundled products, download each search's report from its own row.

If you see **Document not found** with *It may not be ready yet*, the partner marked the case complete before its PDF was ready. Wait a little, click refresh, and try again.

## Duplicate requests

Vitay refuses a second request for the same candidate, partner and category while an earlier one is still open (not yet **Completed** or **Cancelled**). The dialog shows **You have already sent a background check request to this person** and no order is placed. Wait for the open check to finish, pick a different category, or delete the open check first.

Each order is billed, so if a check looks stuck, refresh the tab and ask the candidate to check their inbox before ordering again.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="There's no Background Screening tab on the candidate">
    No partner is active on your account. A root recruiter can request the
    **Background screening** plugin from **Configuration → Plugins**, or
    email hello@vitay.io.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The dialog shows no categories to pick">
    Your account has no screening products set up yet. Contact
    hello@vitay.io.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I submitted and got an error">
    Usually there's already an open check for the same candidate and category
    (see above). Also confirm the candidate has an email address. If it keeps
    failing, contact hello@vitay.io with the candidate's name.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The status hasn't changed in days">
    Click refresh on the tab. If the candidate hasn't answered the partner's
    invitation, ask them to look in their inbox and spam folder for an email
    from Mintz or Validity; Vitay can't resend it. A Mintz check on
    **Candidate submitted** is waiting on Mintz, and one on **On hold
    (information needed)** is waiting on the candidate. Neither needs a new
    request.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The download button is missing or the file won't open">
    The button only appears once the check is completed. If you get the "may
    not be ready yet" message, wait and retry. Reports come straight from the
    partner, so a partner outage also blocks downloads for a while.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
