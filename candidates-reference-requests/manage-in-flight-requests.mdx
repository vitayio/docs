---
title: "Manage in-flight requests"
description: "Resend, cancel, or fix the recipient on an open reference request."
---

After you send a reference request you can remind the candidate, remind a single reference, correct a reference's contact details, or cancel the request.

<Note>
  You can manage requests on any candidate you can open. Editing a reference
  is limited to the candidate's assigned recruiter, the recruiter who
  requested the reference, and root recruiters.
</Note>

## Remind the candidate

Each open request shows as a blue banner at the top of the candidate page:
*Reference request "&lt;question set&gt;" sent to candidate*, with the date.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate">
    Go to **Candidates** and click the candidate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send reminder on the banner">
    Vitay emails the candidate the link again, and texts it if they have a
    mobile number and your firm sends text messages. To send the link
    yourself, click the clipboard icon on the banner to copy it.

    ![SCREENSHOT(manage-in-flight-requests-banner): The open-request banner at the top of a candidate page with the cancel, copy-link and Send reminder buttons](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Remind a single reference

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate's References tab">
    Go to **Candidates**, click the candidate, and stay on the **References**
    tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send reminder on the reference's row">
    The button sits in the **Completed at** column of any reference that
    hasn't answered yet. The same button is on the reference's side panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The button is greyed out when no reminder can be sent:

- **Reminder sent**: a reminder went out in the last 12 hours. Hover it for the time.
- **Reminder limit reached**: 10 reminders have been sent. In the References
  table this case also reads **Reminder sent**.
- **Reminders disabled**: reminders are off for the candidate or the reference.

See
[Reminders and how they work](/candidates-reference-requests/reminders-and-cadence).

## Fix a reference's email or mobile number

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the reference">
    On the candidate's **References** tab, click the reference's row. Its side
    panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the contact details">
    Click the pencil (**Edit reference**), correct the **Email** or **Mobile
    number**, and click **Save**.

    ![SCREENSHOT(manage-in-flight-requests-edit-reference): The Edit Reference form opened from the reference side panel](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a reminder">
    Saving a new email or mobile number cancels the reminders still scheduled
    for that reference, and Vitay doesn't resend anything on its own. Click
    **Send reminder** so the request reaches the new address, then check the
    **Email activity** tab to confirm delivery. See
    [Check email and text delivery](/candidates-reference-requests/email-and-text-delivery).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cancel a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the X on the request's banner">
    Open the candidate and click the **X** button on the open-request banner. Scheduled requests have their own banner (*Reference request
    scheduled*) with the same **X**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Delete">
    The **Delete Request** dialog asks you to confirm. A **Request disabled**
    message confirms the cancellation, and no more reminders go out for it.

    ![SCREENSHOT(manage-in-flight-requests-delete-dialog): The Delete Request confirmation dialog](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

References the candidate already submitted stay on the candidate and can still
be read.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit a reference after they submit?">
    Yes. Contact details stay editable after the reference answers. Only the
    question set is locked once the form has been filled in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="There's no pencil on the reference">
    Only the candidate's assigned recruiter, the recruiter who requested the
    reference, and root recruiters can edit it. It's also hidden on candidates
    from a different sub-account than the one you're viewing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The candidate still isn't getting the email">
    The checks in
    [A reference didn't receive the email](/candidates-reference-requests/reference-not-received)
    apply to candidates too: confirm the address, look for typos, and check
    for a bounce.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
