---
title: "Pre-screening questionnaires"
description: "Send a candidate a pre-screening question set before you check their references, follow their answers and score, and get notified when they complete it."
---

Pre-screening sends a candidate a short questionnaire, built from a question set of type **Pre-screening**, so you can review their answers and score before you start reference checks. Candidates answer on a public form, with no login.

<Note>
  Pre-screening is a plugin the Vitay team activates on your account. When
  it's active, **Pre-Screening** appears in the left sidebar and a
  **Pre-Screening** tab appears on every candidate page. If you don't see
  them, a root recruiter can request the plugin from **Configuration →
  Plugins** or email hello@vitay.io. See
  [Plugins and the marketplace](/account-configuration/plugins).
</Note>

## Create a pre-screening question set

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Question sets">
    Click **Question Sets** in the left sidebar, then the add button to open
    **Add Question Set**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Type to Pre-screening">
    Enter a **Label** and choose **Pre-screening** in the **Type** dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your questions">
    Save, then add questions as for any question set. Rating and matrix
    questions count toward the pre-screening score; other question types show
    as answers only.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only question sets of type Pre-screening are offered when you send a pre-screening request.

## Send a pre-screening request

### From the candidate page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Pre-Screening tab">
    Open the candidate and click the **Pre-Screening** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Pre-screening request">
    The **Send Pre-Screening Request** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a question set and submit">
    Choose a question set. Optionally add **Public notes for the candidate**,
    which the candidate sees on the form. Click **Submit**. A
    **Pre-screening requested** message confirms it.

    ![SCREENSHOT(pre-screening-send-modal): The Send Pre-Screening Request dialog with the question set dropdown and the public notes field](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

### From Quick Request

Click **Quick Request** in the left sidebar and switch to the **Pre-Screening** tab. Enter the candidate's details (or tick **Search existing candidate**), pick the question set and submit. Vitay creates the candidate, sends the request and opens their Pre-Screening tab.

### In bulk from the Candidates list

Select candidates on the **Candidates** list, click **Send new request**, choose **Pre-screening**, pick a question set and confirm. See [Bulk candidate actions](/candidates-reference-requests/bulk-candidate-actions).

Vitay doesn't block duplicates: sending the same question set to the same candidate again creates a second request.

## What the candidate receives

The candidate gets an email from your **Pre screening** email template (default subject: *`{{recruiter_full_name}}` invited you to complete a pre interview questionnaire*). If text messages are on for your account and the candidate has a phone number, they also get a text with the link.

The form greets the candidate by first name and shows your **Pre screening** page text and the questions. After submitting, they see a thank-you page.

You can edit the email, text and page wording under **Configuration → Settings → Emails / Texts / Pages**. The Pre screening entries only appear there while the plugin is active. See [Email, text and page templates](/account-configuration/email-text-and-page-templates).

## Track pre-screenings

- **Pre-Screening page** (left sidebar): every request, with **Title** (question set), **Name**, **Score**, **Assigned user**, **Requested on** and **Completed at**. Use the **Everyone** / **Myself** toggle and the date range to narrow the list, and click a column header to sort.
- **Candidate page:** the **Pre-Screening** tab lists that candidate's requests. Click a row to open it.

## Read the answers or complete on the candidate's behalf

Clicking a pre-screening opens the **Pre-screening answers** page.

While the request is open, the page shows when it was sent, with a **Send reminder** button, and a **Complete pre-screening** button. That button opens the candidate's form in a new tab so you can enter their answers yourself, for example during a phone screen. Submitting it completes the request as if the candidate had.

Once completed, the page shows the score, the completion date and every answer. Use the download button to save a PDF (or DOCX, if enabled for your account), and the eye icon (**Show candidate info**) to include or hide the candidate's contact details in it.

![SCREENSHOT(pre-screening-answers-page): A completed Pre-screening answers page with the score, the download button and the answers](/images/placeholder.png)

## Reminders

- **Automatic:** Vitay schedules three reminders when a pre-screening is sent, spaced by your account's reminder rate (24 hours by default). They go by email, and by text when text messages are on, and stop when the candidate submits. See [Reminders and how they work](/candidates-reference-requests/reminders-and-cadence).
- **Manual:** click **Send reminder** on the Pre-screening answers page. A pre-screening can get at most 10 reminders, at least 12 hours apart. Within 12 hours of the last one the button shows **Reminder sent**; after 10 it shows **Reminder limit reached**. Hover it to see when the last reminder went out.

## Completion notification

When the candidate submits, Vitay sends an email titled *Results for &lt;candidate&gt;'s pre-screening assessment*, with a button that opens the candidate's Pre-Screening tab. Choose who receives it on the **Pre-screening completed** row of **Team notifications** under **Configuration → Settings → Notifications**. New accounts send it to the assigned recruiter. See [Notification settings](/account-configuration/notification-settings).

## Dashboard and export

- **Overview:** the dashboard gets a **Pre-Screening** tab with totals, the completion rate, average completion time and a sent-vs-completed chart. See [Overview dashboard](/reports-lead-generation/overview-dashboard).
- **CSV export:** on the Pre-Screening page, open the filters and click the download icon to export the current list as `pre_screenings.csv`, with question set, candidate name, email, phone, score, assigned recruiter, and created and completed dates. It follows the **Everyone** / **Myself** toggle and date range. Root recruiters can always download; other users can when report downloads are enabled. See [Download reports](/reports-lead-generation/download-reports).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I don't see Pre-screening in the sidebar or on the candidate page">
    The plugin isn't active on your account. A root recruiter can check
    **Configuration → Plugins** and click **Request plugin**, or email
    hello@vitay.io.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The question set dialog has no Type field">
    The **Type** dropdown only appears when the pre-screening or a feedback
    plugin is active. Without it, every set is a reference question set.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The question set dropdown in the request dialog is empty">
    Only question sets of type **Pre-screening** are listed. Create one, or
    change an existing set's type.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Send reminder is greyed out">
    The pre-screening has had 10 reminders, or the last one went out less than
    12 hours ago. Hover the button to see when.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The candidate lost the link">
    Click **Send reminder** to resend it, or use **Complete pre-screening**
    and fill in the form with them on a call.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Nobody got the completion email">
    Check the **Pre-screening completed** row under **Settings →
    Notifications**; the email only goes to the columns ticked there. It's
    sent from your firm's sender address (see
    [Email sender settings](/account-configuration/email-sender-settings#which-sender-your-team-sees)),
    so also check spam filters.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
