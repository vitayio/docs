---
title: "A reference didn't receive the email"
description: "Recruiter steps when a reference says they didn't get the request."
---

When a reference says the request never arrived, check the address, check whether delivery failed, then resend.

<Note>
  These steps are for recruiters. You need access to the candidate to view
  and edit their references.
</Note>

## Step 1: Confirm the email address

A typo in the address is the most common cause.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the reference">
    Go to **Candidates**, open the candidate, and click the reference on the
    **References** tab. Their side panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the address">
    The email address is listed under the reference's name. Compare it with
    the address the reference gives you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Correct it if needed">
    Click the pencil (**Edit reference**), fix the **Email** field and save.
    Then resend the request (Step 3).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2: Check the delivery icon

The envelope icon next to the address reflects the last three months of emails to that address. Hover it for the tooltip:

| Icon | Tooltip | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Green open envelope | **Delivered** | Emails to this address arrived. |
| Red envelope | **Failed** | Delivery failed. |
| Yellow envelope | **Check email validity** | Some emails arrived and some failed. |

For every individual event and the provider's reason, open the reference's **Email activity** tab. See [Check email and text delivery](/candidates-reference-requests/email-and-text-delivery).

![SCREENSHOT(reference-not-received-envelope-icon): The reference side panel with the colored envelope icon next to the email address and its tooltip](/images/placeholder.png)

If delivery failed, confirm the address with the reference by phone or another channel and update it. A failure can also mean a full inbox or a server that rejected the message temporarily. While the last email to an address has bounced, Vitay stops emailing reminders to it; see [What stops reminders](/candidates-reference-requests/reminders-and-cadence#what-stops-reminders).

## Step 3: Resend the request

In the reference's side panel, click **Send reminder**. Vitay sends the request email again, right away, to the address now on file.

## Step 4: Ask them to check spam

If the address is right and nothing failed, the email probably landed in a filter. Ask the reference to:

1. Check their **Spam**, **Junk** or **Promotions** folder.
2. Add the sender to their contacts or safe-senders list.
3. Tell you if they still can't find it, so you can resend.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The address is correct but nothing arrives">
    Their mail server may be blocking Vitay's emails. Ask them to have their
    IT team allow the sender, or use another address.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Send reminder is greyed out">
    Reminders are off for the candidate or reference, the reference already
    has 10 reminders, or one went out in the last 12 hours. See
    [Reminders and how they work](/candidates-reference-requests/reminders-and-cadence).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="They got the email but the link doesn't work">
    Reference links don't expire. A link stops working once the form has been
    submitted, the reference declined, or the reference or request was
    removed. If that doesn't explain it, contact hello@vitay.io with the
    candidate and reference names. Corporate firewalls sometimes block links,
    so a personal device is worth a try.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
