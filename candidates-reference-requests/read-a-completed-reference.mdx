Once a reference submits the question form, you can read their answers and score from the candidate's page.

<Note>
  Any recruiter with access to the candidate can read their references.
</Note>

## Open a completed reference

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate">
    Go to **Candidates** and open the candidate. The page opens on the
    **References** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the reference">
    References are grouped by question set. A completed reference shows its
    date in the **Completed At** column. A reference that hasn't answered
    shows **Send reminder** there instead, and one who declined shows
    **Declined** (only possible when your firm has turned on **References Can
    Decline The Request**). The **Score** column appears when scores are
    shown for your firm.

    ![SCREENSHOT(read-a-completed-reference-references-tab): The candidate's References tab with a question-set group, a completed reference row and the download menu open](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Click the row. The reference's side panel opens with:

    - the score circle, the reference's name, relationship and job title, and
      their contact details;
    - the answers, one card per question.

    ![SCREENSHOT(read-a-completed-reference-panel): A completed reference's side panel with the score circle, contact details and the first answers](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How the score is calculated

Only **rating** and **matrix** answers count. Text, dropdown, radio, checkbox, slider, ranking and date answers never affect the score, and answers marked **N/A** are left out.

- **Rating questions:** the average rating divided by the rating scale. The scale is **5** by default; a root recruiter can switch the firm to **10** with **Default Rating Scale**. An average of 4 out of 5 is 80%.
- **Matrix questions:** each row is scored against the highest column weight on that question. The **N/A** column isn't counted.
- **Both:** when a set has rating and matrix questions, the score is the average of the two results.

For example, with one rating question and one matrix question:

| Answer | Score |
|---|---|
| Rating of 5 out of 5 | 100% |
| Matrix row with the 4th of 5 columns picked | 80% |
| Reference score | 90% |

### The candidate score

The circle at the top of the candidate page averages the scores of the candidate's completed references. Declined references, references switched off with the **Status** toggle, and references with no score are left out.

### Hiding scores

Scores show by default. A root recruiter can hide them on candidate pages, lists and PDF reports by switching off **Show Reference Score** on the **References** settings tab. See [Reference settings](/account-configuration/reference-settings).

The score only reflects what the reference answered. To judge whether the reference is genuine, check the IP-conflict banner and the reference's edits; see [IP conflicts and reference edits](/candidates-reference-requests/ip-conflicts-and-reference-edits).

## Download a report

- **One question set:** on the candidate's **References** tab, each question-set group with at least one completed reference has a download button (**Download report**). Pick **Download PDF**, **Download Word** or **Download CSV**. The eye button next to it (**Show candidate info**) includes or hides the candidate's contact details.
- **One reference:** the reference's side panel has the same download button, with PDF and Word.

To send reports to people outside Vitay, see [Share reports](/candidates-reference-requests/share-reports).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The reference is completed but I see no answers">
    Click the refresh button in the reference's side panel. If the answers
    still don't show, contact hello@vitay.io.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The score seems low">
    Only rating and matrix answers count, so a set made mostly of text or
    choice questions is scored on very few answers. A root recruiter can add
    rating or matrix questions to the set.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't see a score anywhere">
    **Show Reference Score** is switched off for your firm on the
    **References** settings tab.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I need to correct an answer">
    Click the pencil on the answer card (**Edit answer**). You can edit text
    answers and rating values, and the comment on a choice question. The
    option picked on a choice question can't be changed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
