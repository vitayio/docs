Vitay holds personal data on behalf of the hiring firms that use it, mostly what candidates and
references submit during a reference check. Here's what's kept, where, for how long, and how to
remove it.

<Note>
  For data processing agreements, sub-processor lists or compliance questions (GDPR, SOC 2 and
  similar), contact [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
</Note>

## What data Vitay holds

- **Candidates**: name, contact details, and the reference requests your team sends.
- **References**: name, contact details, and their answers.
- **Candidate documents**: files a candidate uploads on the request form when your firm has
  [candidate document upload](/candidates-reference-requests/candidate-documents) on, such as a CV
  or a licence. These can contain more personal detail than the form itself, such as a photo or
  date of birth.
- **Your users**: name, email and role for each person at your firm who uses Vitay.
- **Activity**: a log of actions, such as when a request was sent or answered.

## Data residency

Each region has its own environment, and your data stays in your firm's region:

- United States: `references.vitay.io`
- Canada: `references.vitay.ca`
- United Kingdom: `references.vitay.co.uk`

Customers elsewhere (Australia, for example) are hosted in the US or UK. Contact
[hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) to confirm your region.

## Data retention

By default, nothing expires: Vitay keeps candidate and reference data while your account is
active, and your firm decides when to delete it. Uploaded documents stay with the candidate's
record. The note under the upload area tells candidates to contact your firm for a copy or for
deletion, so make sure whoever handles those requests knows where the files are.

Your firm can also run a retention policy that anonymizes candidate and feedback data after a
period you choose (3 months minimum). Once Vitay enables it, a root recruiter sets it up under
**Configuration → Settings → Data Retention**. See
[Set up automatic data retention](/security-it/data-retention).

## Deleting data

### Single candidates

Deactivating a candidate moves them to the **Recycle Bin**. Nothing is deleted at that point, and
the bin never empties itself. From there, a recruiter can **Reactivate** the candidate, or a root
recruiter can **Delete permanently**, which removes the candidate and their references for good.
See [Recycle Bin](/candidates-reference-requests/recycle-bin).

To erase the personal data but keep the record, use **Purge data** on the candidate, or let the
[retention policy](/security-it/data-retention) do it on a schedule.

Uploaded documents go to the Recycle Bin with the candidate and are removed when the candidate is
permanently deleted. To remove one file sooner, delete it from the candidate's **Documents** tab.

### Your whole account

When an account closes, or on request, Vitay can purge all of a firm's data: answers, requests,
candidates, users and the firm itself. Contact [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) to request
it.

## Security measures

- All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS.
- SAML single sign-on is available; see [Single sign-on (SAML)](/security-it/sso-saml).
- Passwords must meet strength rules, accounts lock after 10 failed attempts, bursts of sign-in and
  reset attempts are slowed, and reset links expire after 1 hour. See
  [Logging in](/getting-started/logging-in).
- Users with MFA enter a code at every password sign-in, and Vitay can require MFA for your whole
  firm. See [Multi-factor authentication & login options](/getting-started/mfa-and-login-options).
- Deactivating a user ends their access straight away.
- Once a reference is submitted, its link can't be used to change the answers.
- Analytics, advertising and support-chat scripts load only after consent. See
  [Cookies and consent](/security-it/cookies-and-consent).
- When Vitay support works in your account, the session is time-limited, recorded, and visible on
  screen. See [Support Mode](/security-it/support-mode).
