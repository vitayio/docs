Vitay handles personal data on behalf of the hiring firms that use it —
primarily the data that candidates and references submit during the reference
checking process. This article explains what data Vitay holds, how long it's
kept, and how it can be deleted.

<Note>
  For formal data processing agreements (DPAs), sub-processor lists, or
  questions about compliance with specific regulations (GDPR, SOC 2, etc.),
  contact [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io). Vitay's legal and
  security teams handle those requests directly.
</Note>

## What data Vitay holds

Vitay stores the following categories of data on behalf of your firm:

- **Candidate data** — name, contact details, and the reference request(s) your
  team creates.
- **Reference data** — name, contact details, and the answers a reference
  submits in response to a reference request.
- **Documents candidates upload** — files a candidate attaches to their
  reference request form when your firm has
  [candidate document upload](/candidates-reference-requests/candidate-documents)
  switched on (for example a CV or résumé, certificates, or a licence). These
  files can carry more personal detail than the form itself, such as a photo,
  date of birth, or nationality, so treat them with the same care as the rest
  of the candidate's record.
- **Recruiter and admin data** — account details (name, email, role) for
  members of your firm who use Vitay.
- **Activity data** — a log of actions taken in the platform (for example, when
  a reference request was sent or a response was received).

## Data residency

Vitay operates separate environments by region:

- **United States** — `references.vitay.io`
- **Canada** — `references.vitay.ca`
- **United Kingdom** — `references.vitay.co.uk`

Data is stored in the region that corresponds to your firm's Vitay environment.
Customers in other regions (for example, Australia) are hosted on the US or UK
instance. Reach out to [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) if you need to
confirm which region your firm is on.

## Data retention

Vitay retains candidate and reference data for as long as your firm's account is
active. By default, nothing expires — retention is governed by your firm's own
policies and any contractual terms with Vitay, and your firm controls when data
is deleted (see below). Documents a candidate uploads are held with that
candidate's record and follow the same rules — they stay for as long as the
candidate does. The notice under the upload area tells candidates that your
firm decides how long their files are kept and that they should contact your
firm to get a copy or ask for deletion, so make sure whoever fields those
requests knows where the files live.

Optionally, your firm can run a **retention policy** that automatically
anonymizes candidate and feedback data after a period you choose (minimum 3
months). Once Vitay has enabled the feature for your account, a root recruiter
configures and runs it themselves from **Configuration → Settings → Data
Retention** — the retention period, whether it cascades to sub-accounts, and
when it last ran. Warning emails go out 30 days ahead, and a **Scheduled for
Anonymization** page lets your team mark individual records **Keep Record**
before the next run. Anonymization is permanent and cannot be undone.

See [Set up automatic data retention](/security-it/data-retention) for the full
walkthrough. If the Data Retention tab is greyed out, contact
[hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) to have the feature switched on.

## Deleting data

### Deleting individual records

Recruiters and admins can deactivate individual candidates and references from
within the Vitay app. A deactivated candidate disappears from the active
application and moves to the **Recycle Bin**, from where any recruiter can
restore them — nothing is deleted at that point.

The Recycle Bin has **no expiry**: candidates stay in it indefinitely, and Vitay
never empties it automatically. Getting a record out of the bin is always a
deliberate act by your team — **Reactivate**, or a root recruiter's **Delete
permanently**, which removes the candidate and everything attached to them and
cannot be undone. See
[Recycle Bin](/candidates-reference-requests/recycle-bin) for the full process.

To erase the personal data on a record while keeping the record itself, use
**Purge data** on the candidate, or let the
[retention policy](/security-it/data-retention) do it on a schedule.

Documents a candidate uploaded move to the Recycle Bin together with the
candidate and are removed for good when the record is fully deleted. To remove
a single file sooner, open the candidate's **Documents** tab and delete it
there — recruiters can do this at any time.

### Full firm data purge

When a firm's account is closed or on request, Vitay can purge all data
associated with that firm. A purge removes data across all related tables —
including answers, reference requests, candidates, recruiters, and the firm
record itself — in dependency order. The process is tracked and auditable
internally, and a rollback capability exists.

If you need to request a full data purge, contact
[hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).

## Security measures

- All data is transmitted over **HTTPS/TLS**.
- Vitay authenticates recruiter sessions with signed tokens and supports
  **SAML SSO** for centralised access control — see
  [Single sign-on (SAML)](/security-it/sso-saml).
- Reference submission links are single-use — once a reference is submitted,
  the link locks and can no longer be used to view or change the response.
- Analytics, advertising, and support-widget scripts load only after the
  visitor gives consent — see
  [Cookies and consent](/security-it/cookies-and-consent).
- Vitay monitors for errors and anomalies via application-level observability
  tooling.
