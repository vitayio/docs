title: "Single sign-on (SAML)"
description: "Let your team log in to Vitay through your identity provider using SAML 2.0 single sign-on."
---

SAML single sign-on (SSO) lets your team sign in to Vitay through your identity provider (IdP),
such as Okta, Microsoft Entra ID or OneLogin, instead of with a Vitay password. Access is then
controlled from your IdP.

<Note>
  Vitay enables SSO per firm; contact [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) to start. After that,
  a root recruiter connects your IdP in Vitay, working with someone who administers the IdP.
</Note>

## Who does what

Vitay's team:

- creates the identity organization behind your account and records the email domains your people
  sign in with (for example `acme.com`);
- turns on **Sign in with SSO** for those domains;
- emails a setup invite to your first administrator. Following it signs them in with root recruiter
  access so they can open the SSO tab before SSO is live;
- can make SSO required for your firm (see below).

You then connect your IdP on the SSO tab.

## The SSO tab

Open **Configuration → Settings → SSO**. The **Single Sign-On** page has three sections:

| Section | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **SSO Connections** | Create the SAML connection and exchange metadata with your IdP. |
| **SCIM Provisioning** | Set up automatic user provisioning; see [SCIM user provisioning](/security-it/scim-provisioning). |
| **Members** | See the people in your Vitay organization and their status. |

![SCREENSHOT(sso-saml-settings-tab): The Single Sign-On tab in Configuration with the SSO Connections, SCIM Provisioning and Members sections](/images/placeholder.png)

The tab appears only after Vitay has created your identity organization, and only root recruiters
can open it.

## How your team signs in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Sign in with SSO">
    It's under the password form on the Vitay login page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your work email">
    Type your work email and click **Continue**. Vitay finds your organization from the email
    domain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your account if asked">
    If several Vitay accounts share your email domain (a parent and its sub-accounts, for example),
    **Choose your account** lists them. Click the right one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with your IdP">
    Vitay sends you to your identity provider, then back to Vitay once you've signed in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An SSO session lasts 24 hours, after which your IdP signs you in again.

## New users and roles

- **Users are created on first sign-in.** If someone signs in through your IdP without a Vitay
  user, Vitay creates one from the email address and name your IdP sends. A deactivated user is
  reactivated instead of duplicated.
- **Roles come from your IdP groups.** The recruiter, root recruiter and supervisor roles are
  granted from the groups mapped on your SCIM connection and follow group changes. Signing in
  through SSO doesn't grant a role on its own. See
  [SCIM user provisioning](/security-it/scim-provisioning).
- **Email addresses must match.** Vitay identifies people by the email your IdP sends. If someone's
  email changes in the IdP, change it in Vitay too.

## Making SSO required

Vitay can make SSO required for your firm. Password sign-ins are then refused with *"Please login
with SSO"*, and **Sign in with SSO** is the only way in. Ask
[hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) to turn this on or off.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="'SSO is not enabled for this email domain'">
    That domain isn't registered for your Vitay account, or SSO isn't on for it yet. Sign in with
    your password for now and send the domain to [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Password sign-in is refused with 'Please login with SSO'">
    Your firm requires SSO. Use **Sign in with SSO**. To get password sign-in back, contact
    [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="'Your account is not linked to Stytch yet'">
    Your user hasn't signed in through the identity provider yet. Sign in with **Sign in with SSO**,
    or use the setup invite Vitay emailed you, then open the tab again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="'Only root users can configure SSO'">
    You need the root recruiter role on this firm. Ask a root recruiter at your firm.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="'No Stytch organization is provisioned for this hiring firm'">
    Vitay hasn't created your identity organization yet. Contact
    [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="'Failed to load Stytch Admin Portal'">
    Sign out, sign back in and open the tab again. If it still fails, contact
    [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Someone assigned in the IdP is rejected">
    Check that the email your IdP sends matches their Vitay user, and that they're assigned to the
    Vitay app in your IdP. An email that already belongs to an unrelated Vitay account can't be
    claimed by your organization; contact [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) if you think
    that's the case.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
