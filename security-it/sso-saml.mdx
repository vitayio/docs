title: "Single sign-on (SAML)"
description: "Let your team log in to Vitay through your identity provider using SAML 2.0 single sign-on."
---

SAML single sign-on (SSO) lets your recruiters log in to Vitay through your
organisation's identity provider (IdP) — such as Okta, Microsoft Entra ID
(Azure AD), or OneLogin — instead of a separate Vitay password. Access is
controlled centrally from your IdP, so joiners, movers, and leavers are handled
in one place.

<Note>
  SSO is enabled per firm by Vitay's team — contact
  [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) to get started. Once it's enabled, the
  IdP connection itself is configured in Vitay by a **root recruiter**, working
  with someone who can administer your IdP.
</Note>

## Who does what

**Vitay's team sets up the groundwork:**

- Creates the identity organisation behind your Vitay account and records the
  **email domains** your people sign in with (for example, `acme.com`).
- Turns on **Sign in with SSO** at the Vitay login page for those domains.
- Emails a **setup invite** to your first administrator. Following that link
  signs them in and gives them root recruiter access, so they can open the SSO
  tab before SSO is live.
- Optionally makes SSO **required** for your firm (see below).

**You configure the connection to your IdP** in Vitay, in the SSO tab described
next.

## The SSO tab in Configuration

Open **Configuration → Settings → SSO**. The tab is titled **Single Sign-On**
and holds three sections:

| Section | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **SSO Connections** | Create and manage the SAML connection between Vitay and your IdP. This is where you exchange the connection details with your IdP and upload or point at your IdP's metadata. |
| **SCIM Provisioning** | Set up automatic user provisioning from your IdP — see [SCIM user provisioning](/security-it/scim-provisioning). |
| **Members** | See the people in your Vitay organisation and their status. |

<Note>
  The SSO tab only appears once Vitay has provisioned your firm's identity
  organisation, and only **root recruiters** can open it. If the tab is greyed
  out or you see *"Only root recruiters can configure SSO"*, contact
  [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) or a root recruiter at your firm.
</Note>

## How your team signs in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Sign in with SSO">
    On the Vitay login page, click **Sign in with SSO** instead of entering a
    password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your work email address">
    Type your work email and click **Continue**. Vitay uses the domain of that
    address to find your organisation — the password field is never involved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your account, if you're asked">
    If more than one Vitay account uses the same email domain (a parent company
    and its sub-accounts, for example), Vitay shows **Choose your account** and
    you pick the right one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate with your IdP">
    You're redirected to your identity provider, sign in there as usual, and
    land back in your Vitay dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A signed-in session lasts **24 hours**, after which your IdP authenticates you
again.

## New accounts and roles

- **Accounts are created on first sign-in.** If someone authenticates through
  your IdP and has no Vitay account yet, Vitay creates one from the details in
  the assertion (email address and name) — no manual setup needed. The same
  happens for someone who was deactivated: the existing account is reactivated
  rather than duplicated.
- **What they can do comes from your IdP groups.** Vitay's roles — recruiter,
  root recruiter, and supervisor — are granted from the IdP groups mapped to
  those roles on your SCIM connection, and are kept in step as group membership
  changes. Signing in via SSO on its own doesn't grant a role. See
  [SCIM user provisioning](/security-it/scim-provisioning).
- **Email addresses must match.** Vitay identifies people by the email address
  your IdP sends. If someone's email changes in your IdP, change it in Vitay
  too.

## Making SSO required

Vitay can mark your firm as **SSO-required**. After that, password sign-in is
refused for your users — anyone who tries gets *"Please login with SSO"* — and
**Sign in with SSO** is the only way in. Ask
[hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) to turn this on or off; it isn't a
setting you can change yourself.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="'SSO is not enabled for this email domain'">
    The domain of the address you entered isn't one of the domains registered
    for your Vitay account, or SSO sign-in hasn't been switched on for it yet.
    Sign in with your password for now and contact
    [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) with the domain you expect to use.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A password login is refused with 'Please login with SSO'">
    Your firm is set to require SSO. Use **Sign in with SSO** on the login page.
    If you need password sign-in restored, contact
    [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="'Your account is not linked to Stytch yet'">
    You opened the SSO tab with an account that hasn't been through the identity
    provider yet. Either sign in via **Sign in with SSO**, or use the setup
    invite Vitay emailed you, and then open the tab again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="'No Stytch organization is provisioned for this hiring firm'">
    Vitay hasn't created the identity organisation for your account yet — that's
    the first step of setup. Contact [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="'Failed to load Stytch Admin Portal'">
    The tab loads the configuration portal using your current session. Sign out,
    sign back in, and open the tab again. If it still fails, contact
    [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Someone is rejected even though they're assigned in the IdP">
    Check that the email address your IdP sends matches the one on their Vitay
    account, and that they're assigned to the Vitay application in your IdP. An
    email address that already belongs to an unrelated Vitay account can't be
    claimed by your organisation — contact
    [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) if you think that's the case.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
