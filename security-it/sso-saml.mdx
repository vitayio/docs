SAML single sign-on (SSO) lets your recruiters log in to Vitay through your
organisation's identity provider (IdP) — such as Okta, Microsoft Entra ID
(Azure AD), or OneLogin — without needing a separate Vitay password. Access is
controlled centrally from your IdP, so joiners, movers, and leavers are handled
in one place.

<Note>
  SSO is configured on a per-firm basis by Vitay's team. Reach out to
  [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) to get started. You will need an
  admin or IT role in your IdP to complete the setup on your side. Using
  Google Workspace? It may be possible on request — contact
  [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
</Note>

## How Vitay's SAML SSO works

Vitay acts as the SAML **service provider (SP)**. Your IdP authenticates your
users and sends a signed SAML assertion back to Vitay. Vitay validates that
assertion and logs the user in — no password is exchanged with Vitay.

Users start from the Vitay login page: they click **Sign in with SSO**, enter
their work email address, and are redirected to your IdP to authenticate
(SP-initiated flow). After your IdP confirms who they are, they land back in
their Vitay account. Vitay uses the email address in the SAML assertion
(`NameID`) to match the user to their Vitay account.

If someone signs in via SSO who doesn't have a Vitay account yet, one is
created for them automatically (just-in-time provisioning) — no manual account
setup is needed.

<Note>
  The user's email address in Vitay must match the email your IdP sends in the
  SAML assertion. If a user's email changes in your IdP, update it in Vitay too.
</Note>

## What you provide to Vitay

To set up SSO, share the following with Vitay's team:

- Your IdP's **SAML metadata URL**. This contains the SSO endpoint and signing
  certificate Vitay uses to validate assertions. If your IdP only gives you a
  metadata XML file, [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) can help.
- The **email domains** that should be covered (for example, `acme.com`).
- Any **login URL or redirect** preferences for your environment.

## What Vitay provides to you

Vitay's team will give you:

- Vitay's **SP metadata** (entity ID and assertion consumer service URL) to
  enter in your IdP when creating the Vitay application.
- Confirmation of which **NameID format** to use — Vitay expects an
  email-address format NameID.

## Security notes

- SAML assertions are **cryptographically signed** by your IdP. Vitay validates
  the signature against your IdP's certificate before granting access.
- Vitay runs `ruby-saml` 1.18, which includes fixes for CVE-2024-45409 and
  related SAML signature-bypass vulnerabilities.
- Sessions expire after 24 hours, requiring re-authentication through your IdP.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A user gets 'Unauthorized access' after being redirected back">
    The email in the SAML assertion doesn't match the email the user entered on
    the Vitay login page, or the assertion failed validation. Confirm your IdP
    is sending the user's work email as the `NameID`, and that the user is
    assigned to the Vitay application in your IdP.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Login worked before but now fails with a signature error">
    Your IdP's signing certificate may have rotated. Provide Vitay's team with
    the updated IdP metadata URL or certificate so they can update the
    configuration.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
