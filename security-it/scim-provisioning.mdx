---
title: "SCIM user provisioning"
description: "Automatically create, update, and deactivate Vitay users from your identity provider using SCIM."
---

SCIM (System for Cross-domain Identity Management) lets your identity provider
(IdP) create and deactivate Vitay accounts for you, and decide what each person
can do in Vitay. Paired with [single sign-on](/security-it/sso-saml), your IT
team manages access to Vitay entirely from the IdP — no manual account creation
or cleanup.

<Note>
  SCIM is configured in **Configuration → Settings → SSO**, which only
  **root recruiters** can open and which appears once Vitay has provisioned your
  firm's identity organisation. Contact
  [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) to get that started. You'll also need
  an admin role in your IdP to configure its SCIM connector.
</Note>

## Where you set it up

In the **SSO** tab, the **SCIM Provisioning** section walks you through creating
the SCIM connection for your IdP and gives you the values to paste into your
IdP's SCIM configuration. The **Members** section on the same tab shows the
people currently in your Vitay organisation, so you can confirm provisioning is
landing as expected.

## What SCIM handles

| Event in your IdP | What happens in Vitay |
|---|---|
| New user assigned to Vitay | A Vitay account is created for that user, or an existing/deactivated one is matched and reactivated |
| User unassigned or deprovisioned | Vitay deactivates the user's account for your firm and revokes their active sessions |
| User added to or removed from a group | Vitay re-checks the user's groups and updates their **role** — recruiter, root recruiter, or supervisor |

Changes are processed in the background as your IdP sends them, so they
normally take effect within moments rather than instantly.

### Roles come from your groups

Vitay manages three roles through SCIM: **recruiter**, **root recruiter**, and
**supervisor**. A user gets exactly the roles that their mapped IdP groups grant
them:

- Add someone to a mapped group and they gain that role in Vitay.
- Remove them from it and Vitay removes the role again.
- Root recruiter and supervisor both include the base recruiter role
  automatically.

Vitay has no group concept of its own — IdP groups are used only to decide which
Vitay role each person gets. If you're not sure how your groups map to Vitay
roles, ask [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).

### Deactivation and reactivation

When your IdP deprovisions someone, Vitay deactivates their access to your firm
and immediately revokes their signed-in sessions, so an open browser tab can't
keep working. If that was their only Vitay firm, the account itself is
deactivated too.

Reassigning the person later brings the **same** account back — their history in
Vitay is preserved and no duplicate is created. Their roles are re-applied from
whichever groups they're in at that point.

<Note>
  Profile updates (name, email) do **not** sync to existing Vitay users. After
  an account exists, change a name or email in Vitay itself.
</Note>

<Note>
  Each SCIM connection provisions into one Vitay account. If your organisation
  uses several Vitay firms (a parent and sub-accounts, for example), Vitay's team
  will confirm which firm each connection maps to.
</Note>

## What you provide to Vitay

Most of the setup happens in the SSO tab and in your IdP, but it helps to agree
up front on:

- Your IdP's **SCIM connector type** (for example, Okta or Microsoft Entra ID).
- The **groups** you want provisioned into Vitay, and which Vitay role
  (recruiter, root recruiter, or supervisor) each group should grant.
- The **attribute mappings** your IdP needs to identify people — typically first
  name, last name, and email address.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A user was deprovisioned in the IdP but can still log in to Vitay">
    Deprovisioning is processed in the background, so there can be a short delay
    before it takes effect. If access persists beyond a few minutes, contact
    [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A new user isn't appearing in Vitay after being assigned in the IdP">
    Check that the SCIM connector in your IdP shows a successful push for that
    user with no error status, and that the user has an email address. Share the
    IdP error log with [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) if the issue
    persists.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A user was provisioned but can't do anything in Vitay">
    They have an account but no role yet. Roles come from your mapped IdP
    groups — add the user to the group for the Vitay role they need, and Vitay
    updates them on the next sync.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A user's name or email updated in the IdP but not in Vitay">
    This is expected — profile updates don't sync to existing Vitay users. Make
    the change in Vitay itself, or ask
    [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) for help.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A role keeps coming back (or disappearing) after being changed in Vitay">
    For SCIM-provisioned users, your IdP groups are the source of truth: Vitay
    re-applies the roles your groups grant and removes the ones they don't.
    Change the person's group membership in your IdP rather than the role in
    Vitay.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
