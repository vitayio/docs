---
title: "SCIM user provisioning"
description: "Automatically create, update, and deactivate Vitay users from your identity provider using SCIM."
---

SCIM lets your identity provider (IdP) create and deactivate Vitay users and decide their roles.
Combined with [single sign-on](/security-it/sso-saml), your IT team manages Vitay access entirely
from the IdP.

<Note>
  SCIM is set up in **Configuration → Settings → SSO**. Only root recruiters can open that tab, and
  it appears once Vitay has created your firm's identity organization; contact
  [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) to start. You also need admin rights in your IdP.
</Note>

## Where you set it up

On the **SSO** tab, the **SCIM Provisioning** section creates the SCIM connection and gives you the
values to paste into your IdP. The **Members** section on the same tab lists the people in your
Vitay organization, so you can check that provisioning works.

## What SCIM handles

| In your IdP | In Vitay |
|---|---|
| User assigned to Vitay | A user is created, or an existing or deactivated one is matched and reactivated |
| User unassigned or deprovisioned | The user is deactivated for your firm and signed out |
| User added to or removed from a group | The user's role is updated |

Changes are processed as your IdP sends them and usually take effect within moments.

### Roles come from groups

SCIM manages three roles: recruiter, root recruiter and supervisor. Each user gets exactly the
roles their mapped IdP groups grant. Add someone to a mapped group and they gain the role; remove
them and Vitay removes it. Root recruiter and supervisor both include the recruiter role.

IdP groups only decide roles; Vitay has no groups of its own. If you're unsure how your groups map
to roles, ask [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).

### Deactivation and reactivation

When your IdP deprovisions someone, Vitay deactivates them for your firm and ends their signed-in
sessions. If that was their only Vitay firm, the whole user is deactivated.

Assigning them again brings back the same user, with their history, and re-applies roles from their
current groups.

Name and email changes in your IdP don't update existing Vitay users. Change those in Vitay.

Each SCIM connection provisions into one Vitay firm. If you use a parent and sub-accounts, Vitay
will confirm which firm each connection maps to.

## What to agree with Vitay up front

- Your IdP's SCIM connector type (Okta or Microsoft Entra ID, for example).
- The groups to provision, and which Vitay role each one grants.
- The attributes your IdP sends to identify people, usually first name, last name and email.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A deprovisioned user can still sign in">
    There can be a short delay. If access lasts more than a few minutes, contact
    [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A newly assigned user doesn't appear in Vitay">
    Check that your IdP shows a successful push for that user with no error, and that the user has
    an email address. If it still fails, send the IdP error log to
    [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A provisioned user can't do anything">
    They have no role yet. Add them to the IdP group for the role they need.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A name or email change in the IdP didn't reach Vitay">
    That's expected. Make the change in Vitay.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A role I changed in Vitay keeps reverting">
    For SCIM users, IdP groups decide roles. Change the person's group membership in your IdP
    instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
