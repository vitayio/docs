title: "SCIM user provisioning"
description: "Automatically provision and deprovision Vitay users from your identity provider using SCIM."
---

SCIM (System for Cross-domain Identity Management) lets your identity provider
(IdP) automatically create and deactivate Vitay user accounts. When paired
with [SSO](/security-it/sso-saml), your IT team manages who has access to
Vitay entirely from the IdP — no manual account creation or cleanup needed.

<Note>
  SCIM provisioning is configured with Vitay's team as part of your SSO setup.
  Contact [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) to enable it. You will
  need an admin role in your IdP to configure the SCIM connector on your side.
</Note>

## What SCIM handles

| Event in your IdP | What happens in Vitay |
|---|---|
| New user assigned to Vitay | A Vitay account is created or activated for that user |
| User unassigned or deprovisioned | Vitay deactivates the user's account and revokes their active sessions |
| User added to or removed from a group | Vitay updates the user's **role** (recruiter, root recruiter, or supervisor) based on the group-to-role mapping configured at setup |

<Note>
  Profile updates (name, email) do **not** currently sync to existing Vitay
  users. After an account is created, name or email changes are made in Vitay
  itself.
</Note>

Vitay has no separate group concept — IdP groups are used only to determine
which Vitay role a user gets, via the mapping agreed with Vitay's team during
setup.

<Note>
  Vitay provisions users into the hiring firm associated with your SCIM
  configuration. If your organisation uses multiple Vitay firms (for example, a
  parent and sub-accounts), Vitay's team will confirm which firm each SCIM
  connector maps to.
</Note>

## How it works

Vitay listens for SCIM events from your IdP (delivered via webhook). Each event
is recorded and processed asynchronously:

- **Create** — when a new member is assigned, Vitay creates the account. If the
  user first logs in via SSO before the SCIM event arrives, Vitay handles that
  gracefully — both flows simply create or match the same user.
- **Group change** — when a user's group membership changes, Vitay re-syncs
  their role according to your group-to-role mapping.
- **Delete / deactivate** — when a user is removed in your IdP, Vitay
  deactivates their account and revokes their active sessions so they can no
  longer log in.

## What you provide to Vitay

To set up SCIM, share the following with Vitay's team:

- Your IdP's **SCIM connector type** (for example, Okta or Microsoft Entra ID).
- The **groups** you want provisioned into Vitay, and which Vitay role
  (recruiter, root recruiter, or supervisor) each group should map to.
- Any **attribute mappings** your IdP requires to correctly identify users
  (typically first name, last name, and email).

Vitay's team will provide the **SCIM endpoint URL** and **bearer token** to
enter in your IdP's SCIM configuration.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A user was deprovisioned in the IdP but can still log in to Vitay">
    SCIM events are processed asynchronously. There may be a short delay between
    an IdP change and the deactivation taking effect in Vitay. If access persists
    beyond a few minutes, contact [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A new user isn't appearing in Vitay after being assigned in the IdP">
    Confirm that the SCIM connector in your IdP shows a successful push for the
    user (no error status). Share the IdP error log with Vitay's team if the
    issue persists.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A user's name or email updated in the IdP but not in Vitay">
    This is expected — profile updates from your IdP don't currently sync to
    existing Vitay users. Make the name or email change in Vitay itself, or ask
    [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) for help.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
