title: "Support Mode: when Vitay staff access your account"
description: "What the Viewing as banner means, how long a Vitay support session lasts, what is recorded about it, and what support staff can and cannot do while it is running."
---

When you raise a support ticket, a Vitay support engineer may need to see your account exactly as you see it. **Support Mode** lets them do that: they open a time-limited, audited session inside your account instead of asking you for your password. This article explains what that looks like, what is recorded, and what the limits are.

<Note>
  This article is for recruiters and IT reviewers. Support Mode is started by Vitay staff, not by anyone in your firm — there is nothing to switch on, and no setting to configure.
</Note>

## The Support Mode banner

While a support session is running, a coloured bar is pinned to the top of every page of the app:

- **Support Mode Active** in bold on the left,
- **Viewing as** followed by the email address of the Vitay staff member running the session,
- a **Session expires in Xm YYs** countdown that ticks down every second,
- an **Exit Support Mode** button.

The banner cannot be dismissed and is visible on every screen for as long as the session lasts, so there is never a state where someone is in your account without it being on screen.

## How long a session lasts

A support session lasts a **maximum of one hour** and cannot be extended — the session token is minted with a one-hour expiry and refreshing it is blocked, so a longer investigation means starting a fresh, separately audited session.

A session ends when:

- the staff member clicks **Exit Support Mode**, which closes the session and revokes the token immediately;
- the one-hour countdown reaches zero — the app exits automatically, and any further request with that token is rejected as *"Impersonation session invalid or expired"*.

## What is recorded

Every support session is written to an audit record before it starts. Vitay stores:

- who opened it (the staff member) and whose account it was opened on;
- a **written reason**, which is mandatory and must be between 10 and 500 characters — a session cannot be started without one;
- a support ticket reference, where one was supplied;
- the IP address and browser the staff member used;
- when the session started, when it is set to expire, and when it actually ended;
- a running count of the actions performed during the session;
- how the session ended, and its final state (ended or expired).

Alongside the session record, entries are written to the activity trail when a session **starts**, when it **ends** (with the duration and the number of actions), and when an attempt is **denied**.

If you need a report of the support sessions opened on your account, ask [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).

## What support staff can and cannot do

While in Support Mode the staff member sees the app with your user's permissions — the same candidates, references, reports and settings that user can reach. Several actions are blocked outright, whatever the user's role:

| Blocked during Support Mode | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Changing the account password | Support can never lock you out of your own account or set a password they know. |
| Changing multi-factor authentication settings | MFA cannot be disabled or re-enrolled from a support session. |
| Changing the account's email address | The account identity cannot be moved. |
| Deleting the user account | Irreversible account removal is off the table. |
| Refreshing the session token | The one-hour limit is hard; a session cannot quietly extend itself. |

Vitay also restricts who can start a session and on whom:

- only Vitay administrators can start one, and they cannot start one on another administrator;
- they cannot start a session on themselves, on an inactive account, or on a user with no hiring firm;
- a support session cannot launch another support session — no chaining;
- each administrator is capped at 20 sessions in any 24-hour period.

Password expiry rules are not applied inside a support session, because password changes are blocked there anyway.

<Warning>
  Support Mode does not restrict what your user's role can already *see*. If the user is a root recruiter, the session can reach everything a root recruiter can reach, including candidate records and uploaded documents. Where that matters for your firm's policies, say so on the ticket — and see [Data and privacy](/security-it/data-and-privacy) for how the underlying data is handled.
</Warning>

## Asking about a session

If you saw the banner and want to know why, or you want Support Mode excluded from your account, email [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) with the date and time you saw it. Every session has a stored reason and, usually, a ticket reference, so Vitay can tell you exactly what it was for.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I saw the banner but I didn't ask for support">
    Sessions are usually opened against a support ticket raised by someone in your firm — it may have been a colleague. Contact [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) with the time you saw the banner and the email address shown in it, and Vitay can look up the recorded reason.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The banner is covering the top of the app">
    The banner is pinned to the top of the window on purpose. It disappears as soon as the session ends — either when the staff member exits or when the countdown reaches zero.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Support Mode change anything in our audit history?">
    No — actions taken during a session are recorded like any other action, and the session itself is recorded separately with who opened it and why. Nothing is hidden or attributed to a fake user.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
