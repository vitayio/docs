title: "Support Mode: when Vitay staff access your account"
description: "What the Viewing as banner means, how long a Vitay support session lasts, what is recorded about it, and what support staff can and cannot do while it is running."
---

When you raise a support ticket, a Vitay support engineer may need to see your account as you see
it. Support Mode lets them open a time-limited, recorded session in a user's account without
asking for a password.

<Note>
  For recruiters and IT reviewers. Only Vitay staff can start Support Mode; there's nothing to set
  up on your side.
</Note>

## The Support Mode banner

During a session, a coloured bar is pinned to the top of every page. It shows **Support Mode
Active**, **Viewing as** with the email of the Vitay staff member, a **Session expires in**
countdown, and an **Exit Support Mode** button. It can't be dismissed, so nobody is ever in your
account without the banner on screen.

![SCREENSHOT(support-mode-banner): The Support Mode banner across the top of the app with the Viewing as email, the countdown and the Exit Support Mode button](/images/placeholder.png)

## How long a session lasts

Sessions last 1 hour by default. For longer work, such as an onboarding call, the staff member can
choose 2 or 4 hours when they start. The length can't be extended later; more time means starting a
new, separately recorded session.

A session ends when the staff member clicks **Exit Support Mode**, or when the countdown reaches
zero. Either way, its access stops at once.

## Users with more than one account

If the user can work in several accounts (a parent and its sub-accounts, for example), the staff
member can use the company switcher like the user can, but only for accounts the user already has.
The banner, countdown and expiry stay the same, and each switch is recorded.

## What is recorded

Before a session starts, Vitay records:

- who opened it and on whose account;
- a written reason (required, 10 to 500 characters);
- the support ticket reference, when there is one;
- the staff member's IP address and browser;
- the chosen length, start time, expiry and actual end time;
- the number of actions taken, and how the session ended.

The activity log also gets an entry when a session starts, when the staff member switches account,
when it ends (with its length and action count), and when an attempt is refused.

To get a report of support sessions on your account, ask [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).

## What support staff can and can't do

The staff member sees the app with the user's permissions. These actions are blocked during Support
Mode, whatever the user's role:

| Blocked | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Changing the user's password | Support can't lock you out or set a password they know. |
| Changing MFA settings | MFA can't be turned off or re-enrolled. |
| Changing the user's email | The user's identity can't be moved. |
| Deleting the user | No irreversible account removal. |
| Downloading candidate-uploaded documents | Staff can see a document exists, but not open it. |

Vitay also limits who can start a session and on whom:

- only Vitay administrators can start one, and never on another administrator, on themselves, or on
  an inactive user;
- a session can't start another session;
- each administrator can start at most 20 sessions in 24 hours.

<Warning>
  Apart from the actions above, Support Mode can reach whatever the user's role can see. On a root
  recruiter's account, that includes all candidate records and settings. If that matters for your
  firm's policies, say so on the ticket. See [Data and privacy](/security-it/data-and-privacy).
</Warning>

## Asking about a session

If you saw the banner and want to know why, or want Support Mode excluded from your account, email
[hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) with the date and time. Every session has a recorded reason
and usually a ticket reference.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I saw the banner but didn't ask for support">
    A colleague may have raised the ticket. Send [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) the time
    you saw the banner and the email shown in it, and Vitay will look up the recorded reason.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The banner covers the top of the app">
    That's deliberate. It goes away when the session ends.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Support Mode hide anything from our activity history?">
    No. Actions during a session are recorded like any others, and the session itself is recorded
    separately with who opened it and why.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
