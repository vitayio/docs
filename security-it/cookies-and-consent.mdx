Vitay asks every visitor for consent before it loads analytics, advertising or support-chat scripts
and the cookies they set. Use this page to answer your privacy team's questions or to change your
own choice.

<Note>
  No role is needed. The banner and preferences are available to everyone, including candidates and
  reference providers without a Vitay login.
</Note>

## How consent works

On a first visit, a banner at the bottom of the page offers three choices:

- **Accept all** allows every category.
- **Reject non-essential** allows only strictly necessary cookies.
- **Customize** opens **Cookie preferences**, where you choose per category.

Nothing outside the strictly necessary category loads until consent is given. After you choose, a
**Cookie preferences** button stays on every page so you can change your mind.

## Cookie categories

| Category | What it covers | Can be turned off |
|---|---|---|
| **Strictly necessary** | Sign-in and SSO sessions, language, white-label branding, bot protection, Stripe payment and fraud checks, and your consent choice | No |
| **Functional** | The in-app support chat and Google address suggestions on public forms | Yes |
| **Analytics** | Usage statistics, loaded through Google Tag Manager | Yes |
| **Advertising** | Campaign measurement pixels | Yes |

Click **View cookies** on a category to see each cookie's **Name**, **Provider**, **Purpose** and
**Duration**. Scripts that set no cookie but send the visitor's IP address to their provider, such
as Google address suggestions, are listed too.

With Functional off, address fields are plain text fields, and the sidebar **Help** link opens your
cookie preferences instead of the chat.

## Where your choice applies

- **Across Vitay sites**: the choice is stored on your firm's Vitay domain, so it covers the
  reference app, Configuration and the feedback site. A white-label domain asks separately.
- **For 12 months**: after that, or when Vitay changes its cookie list, you're asked again.
- **In the Bullhorn custom tab**: the banner appears the first time you open the tab and your
  choice is remembered there. It's stored separately for each ATS site. Until you choose, only
  strictly necessary cookies are used.
- **In embedded forms and Configuration previews**: no banner is shown, and only strictly necessary
  cookies are used.

## Change or withdraw consent

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Cookie preferences">
    Click the **Cookie preferences** button on any Vitay page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch categories on or off">
    Click **View cookies** first if you want to see what a category covers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save preferences">
    If you withdrew a category you'd allowed before, the page reloads so those scripts stop. Cookies
    they already set expire on their own schedule; clear them in your browser if you want them gone
    now.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The banner keeps coming back">
    Your choice is stored in a cookie. A private window, a browser set to clear cookies on close, or
    an extension that clears site data removes it. Allow cookies for your Vitay domain; see
    [Supported browsers](/security-it/browser-support).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Address suggestions don't appear on a form">
    They're in the Functional category. Type the address by hand, or open **Cookie preferences**,
    switch Functional on and save. Suggestions start without a reload.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The support chat disappeared">
    The chat is in the Functional category. Switch it on in **Cookie preferences**, or click
    **Help** in the sidebar, which opens the preferences when the chat isn't loaded.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Our privacy team needs the full cookie list">
    Every cookie is listed in **Cookie preferences** under **View cookies**. For the privacy policy,
    DPA and sub-processor list, see [Data and privacy](/security-it/data-and-privacy) or contact
    [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
