Vitay asks every visitor for cookie consent before it loads its analytics,
advertising, or support-widget scripts and the cookies they set. This article
explains the cookie categories, how consent is stored, and how to change it —
useful when your IT or privacy team asks what Vitay drops on a browser.

<Note>
  No role is required. The consent banner and preferences are available to
  everyone, including candidates and reference providers who have no Vitay
  login.
</Note>

## How consent works

The first time someone visits Vitay, a banner appears at the bottom of the page
with three choices:

- **Accept all** — allow every category.
- **Reject non-essential** — allow only strictly necessary cookies.
- **Customize** — open the **Cookie preferences** modal and choose per category.

Nothing in the Functional, Analytics, or Advertising categories loads until
consent is given. Rejecting is exactly as prominent and as quick as accepting.

Once a choice is made, the banner is replaced by a persistent **Cookie
preferences** control that stays available on every page, so consent can be
changed or withdrawn at any time.

## Cookie categories

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Strictly necessary — always on">
    Cookies Vitay cannot function without: your sign-in session and single
    sign-on session, two-factor authentication state (the cookie that remembers
    a trusted device), your language preference, cached white-label branding,
    bot protection, the Stripe payment and fraud-prevention cookies, and the
    cookie that records your consent choice itself. These cannot be switched
    off, and Vitay sets them whether or not consent is given.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Functional">
    Cookies that support optional conveniences rather than core functionality —
    notably the in-app support chat widget. Turning this category off means the
    widget is not loaded; the **Help** entry in the sidebar opens your cookie
    preferences instead so you can re-enable it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Analytics">
    Product analytics used to understand how Vitay is used in aggregate. Loaded
    through Google Tag Manager, and only once analytics consent is granted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Advertising">
    Marketing and audience-measurement pixels used to measure campaign
    effectiveness. Loaded only once advertising consent is granted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Click **View cookies** on any category to see every cookie it declares, with
its **Name**, **Provider**, **Purpose**, and **Duration** — so there's no need
to inspect the browser to answer a privacy questionnaire.

## Where consent applies

<Steps>
  <Step title="One choice across the Vitay sites you use">
    Consent is stored in a first-party cookie on your firm's Vitay domain, so a
    choice made on the reference-checking site also applies to the configuration
    and feedback sites. Firms on a white-label domain give consent separately on
    that domain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remembered for 12 months">
    Vitay re-asks after 12 months. Vitay also re-asks everyone when it makes a
    material change to its cookie list, so a stored choice doesn't silently
    cover new cookies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Not shown inside ATS embeds">
    When Vitay is embedded inside an ATS (for example a Bullhorn tab), no
    consent banner is shown and no analytics, advertising, or functional
    cookies are set at all — regardless of any consent given elsewhere. The
    embed uses strictly necessary cookies only.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change or withdraw consent

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open cookie preferences">
    Click the **Cookie preferences** control on any Vitay page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the categories">
    Switch categories on or off. Click **View cookies** on a category first if
    you want to see exactly which cookies it covers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save preferences**. If you have withdrawn consent for a category
    that was previously granted, the page reloads so the affected scripts stop
    loading and no further data is collected. Cookies those services already
    set are not deleted by Vitay — they expire on their own schedule, though
    you can clear them from your browser immediately if you prefer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The banner keeps coming back">
    The consent choice is stored in a cookie. If the browser is set to clear
    cookies on close, is in a private window, or has an extension that clears
    site data, the choice cannot persist and the banner will reappear on each
    visit. Allow cookies from your Vitay domain — see
    [Browser support](/security-it/browser-support).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The support chat widget disappeared">
    The in-app support chat widget sits in the Functional category. If that
    category is off, the widget is not loaded. Open **Cookie preferences**,
    switch Functional on, and save — or use the sidebar **Help** entry, which
    opens the preferences modal when the widget is unavailable.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Our privacy team needs the full cookie list">
    Every cookie Vitay declares is listed in the preferences modal, grouped by
    category, with its purpose and lifetime. For the privacy policy, DPA
    template, and sub-processor list, see
    [Data and privacy](/security-it/data-and-privacy) or contact
    [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
