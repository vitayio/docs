Vitay runs in the browser. There's nothing to install.

## Supported browsers

Use a current version of one of these, on desktop or mobile:

- Google Chrome (including Chrome for Android)
- Microsoft Edge
- Mozilla Firefox
- Apple Safari (macOS and iOS)

Internet Explorer doesn't work with Vitay. Browsers built on old versions of Chromium may not work
either; use Chrome or Edge instead.

## Browser settings Vitay needs

- **JavaScript** must be on, or the app won't load.
- **Cookies** must be allowed for your Vitay domain. Vitay uses cookies to keep you signed in and to
  remember your cookie consent choice. Blocking them can break SSO sign-in, and a browser that
  clears cookies on close shows the consent banner on every visit. See
  [Cookies and consent](/security-it/cookies-and-consent).
- **Local and session storage** must be available. Vitay uses them to keep your place between page
  loads.

## For IT teams

- Keep browser auto-updates on so users stay on current versions.
- Vitay needs no browser extensions.
- If a proxy, firewall or TLS inspection tool filters traffic, allow `*.vitay.io`, `*.vitay.ca`
  (Canada) and `*.vitay.co.uk` (United Kingdom).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Vitay shows a blank page or won't load">
    Check that JavaScript and cookies are on. Try a private or incognito window to rule out an
    extension. If it only happens on managed devices, check whether your proxy or security tools
    block `*.vitay.io`, `*.vitay.ca` or `*.vitay.co.uk`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="SSO sign-in sends me back to the login page">
    This is usually a blocked cookie. Allow cookies for your Vitay domain and your identity
    provider's domain. See [Single sign-on (SAML)](/security-it/sso-saml).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The app looks broken on one device">
    Update the browser, clear its cache and reload. Try a private window to rule out extensions such
    as ad blockers. If it still looks wrong, email [hello@vitay.io](mailto:hello@vitay.io) with the
    browser name and version and what you see.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
